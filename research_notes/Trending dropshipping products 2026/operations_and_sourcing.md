## 1. Major supplier platforms in 2026 and how their models, pricing and US/EU warehouse networks changed after tariffs and the end of de minimis

### Takeaway
The China-to-consumer small-parcel model that defined dropshipping until 2024 is structurally broken in both the US and the EU. The US $800 de minimis exemption has been suspended for all countries since August 29, 2025, and CBP made the suspension indefinite on June 24, 2026. The EU now charges a €3-per-item duty on parcels worth €150 or less (since July 1, 2026), with a €2-per-item handling fee expected from November 1, 2026. As a result, CJdropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket, AliExpress, Temu, Printful and Printify have all moved toward stock held in US or EU warehouses (bulk import, then local fulfilment) or local production. The US tariff stack moved sharply during 2026. IEEPA tariffs were struck down in February. A 10% Section 122 surcharge ran from February to July. Since July 24, 2026, new Section 301 "forced-labor" tariffs of 10–12.5% have applied, stacked on top of the legacy China Section 301 duties.

### Inferences
- By late 2026, the operative split is no longer "AliExpress vs. agent". It is "who holds the import and the inventory". There are four tiers: (a) suppliers with US/EU stock (CJ US/EU warehouses, Zendrop US, Spocket, BigBuy, Temu/AliExpress local sellers); (b) local production (POD); (c) Western wholesale or branded dropship; and (d) residual China direct-ship, which is viable mainly for the UK until 2028 and for high-margin, light items elsewhere.
- Warehouse programs need minimum stock commitments (CJ: 10 pcs per variant). That pushes "dropshippers" toward a light-inventory, private-label model and erodes the zero-inventory premise.
- The US tariff layer is unstable: three legal regimes in nine months, with live litigation over Section 301 and possible refunds. Sellers should treat landed cost as volatile and reprice often. Holding US stock bought under a higher tariff regime carries inventory risk if rates fall.
- The EU and UK are now on a delayed version of the US path. The EU is at a flat €3 + €2 per item until 2028 and then full duty. The UK keeps duty relief until 2028 but already requires seller-collected VAT. For EU/UK-focused sellers, the duty cliff is smaller in percentage terms than the US one. The per-item fees fall disproportionately on cheap, multi-item orders.

## 2. 2026 shipping times and costs (China vs. US/EU warehouses vs. Mexico/Vietnam) and how delivery expectations and platform rules affect product choice

### Takeaway
Domestic or in-region warehouse fulfilment delivers in roughly 3–8 days in the US and 1–7 business days within the EU. China direct now means DDP air at about 6–12 days to the US, or 7–23 days (median about 11) to the EU in independent tracking data. Each China-to-US parcel also carries duties and entry or brokerage charges. Platforms increasingly reward fast, accurate delivery promises: TikTok Shop US expects shipping within 2 business days and delivery within about 6, and Google Merchant Center validates delivery estimates. Both favour products that can be stocked locally.

## 3. Typical 2026 margins, ad costs (brief), return rates and chargebacks; what viable unit economics look like

### Takeaway
Margins are thin. At median Meta efficiency (ROAS of about 1.9x) and the classic 3x landed-cost markup, contribution before returns and fees is only about 15% of revenue. Viability in 2026 therefore depends on (a) a higher AOV or bundles, (b) gross margins of 65–70%+ after duties, (c) organic or affiliate traffic (TikTok) or repeat purchase to amortise CAC, and (d) tight control of returns (about 19–25% average online, higher in apparel) and chargebacks. The chargeback limit matters more since Visa's "Excessive" threshold dropped to 1.5% in April 2026. EU sellers carry structurally higher return exposure because of the 14-day withdrawal right and the new withdrawal button (see Q5).

The exact source among these vendor pages could not be isolated. Treat as an anecdotal vendor claim. — [sticky.io](https://www.sticky.io/insights/post/is-dropshipping-dead-or-still-profitable-in-2026-with-new-us-tariffs); [DailyFulfill](https://www.dailyfulfill.com/dropshipping-in-usa-2026-all-you-should-know/); [TechTraq](https://www.techtraq.com/blog/real-cost-dropshipping-2026)

### Inferences
- **Illustrative unit economics (inference from the cited medians, not a sourced benchmark).** Take a $40 product with a 3x markup, so landed cost (product + duty + domestic shipping) is about $13.30, or 33%. At ROAS 1.93, ad spend is about 52% of revenue. That leaves about 15% before payment fees (~3%), returns (19–25% of orders refunded, part of which is unrecoverable), apps and chargebacks, so net is in the low single digits. This matches the vendor "single-digit" claim.
- **A viable 2026 profile likely needs:**
  - AOV of about $50–80+, via bundles or multi-packs.
  - A landed-cost ratio of 25% or less of price, i.e. 4x+ markup, after duties.
  - Some traffic not bought at Meta CPA (TikTok affiliates or creators, organic, email/SMS).
  - Repeat purchase, so that LTV:CAC clears.
  - Return rate held well below the apparel average, which argues against fit-dependent products.
- The 1.5% Visa VAMP threshold makes slow delivery, "where is my order" disputes and item-not-as-described claims (the classic China-dropship failure modes) an existential risk to payment processing. Fast local fulfilment is partly a chargeback-control choice.
- **EU/UK:** the €3 + €2 per-item fees on China-shipped low-value goods add about €5 per item. That is about 17% of a €30 item, so cheap items shipped from China into the EU lose most of their margin. EU-stocked or locally produced items avoid this.

## 5. Compliance requirements that matter in 2026 (US; EU; UK)

### Takeaway
2026 brought compliance into the import transaction itself. In the US, CPSC certificate data must be e-filed at entry from July 8, 2026, including for low-value shipments. Postal duties must be prepaid by carriers or qualified parties. MoCRA cosmetics registration is in its first renewal cycle. In the EU, the €3 duty and €2 handling fee apply. IOSS still governs VAT on consignments of €150 or less. GPSR requires an EU responsible person and product-safety information on every listing. A mandatory online "withdrawal button" took effect on June 19, 2026. In the UK, VAT is collected at the point of sale on consignments of £135 or less, and the customs-duty relief ends by October 2028. Whoever is the importer of record now carries real liability, and in a warehouse model that is often the seller or its supplier.

### Cited Findings

**US**
- **CPSC eFiling.** Mandatory from July 8, 2026. Importers must file certificate-of-compliance data in CBP's ACE at entry for 2,400+ HTS codes. The requirement covers de minimis/low-value shipments, and CPSC says it will expand surveillance of low-value e-commerce shipments. It extends to foreign-trade-zone entries from January 8, 2027. Non-compliant shipments face holds and storage fees, and CPSC may ask CBP to seize them. Product-Registry or "Reference" message sets are available for repeat products. — [CPSC news release](https://cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/CPSC-Implements-Mandatory-eFiling-for-Certificates-of-Compliance-Targeting-Dangerous-Foreign-Imports); [Foley & Lardner](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/05/cpsc-efiling-begins-july-2026importers-of-consumer-products-are-you-ready/); [GDLSK](https://www.gdlsk.com/new-cpsc-efiling-rule-for-imported-consumer-products-effective-july-8-2026/); [Arnold & Porter](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/blogs/consumer-products-and-retail-navigator/2026/03/ready-set-upload-meeting-cpscs-efiling-deadline-without-losing-your-cool); [FedEx CPSC FAQ](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/meisa-middle-east/downloads/fedex-cpsc-external-faqs-en-meisa.pdf)
- **Postal and de minimis entry.** Duties on mail shipments must be paid by the international carrier or a CBP-certified qualified party, not collected on delivery. See Q1 for details. — [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/cbp-suspends-de-minimis-exemption-and-introduces-new-postal-entry-requirements)
- **MoCRA (cosmetics).** It requires facility registration and product listing and gives FDA mandatory-recall, records-access and registration-suspension powers. Failure to register is a prohibited act under FD&C Act §301(hhh). A vendor blog reports 14,299 active facility registrations and 992,907 product listings as of January 6, 2026. The first biennial renewal date is disputed: July 1, 2026 (Wiley) versus February 20, 2026 for February 2024 registrants. On September 9, 2026, FDA clarified that it does not issue registration "certificates" (a scam vector). Final cosmetics GMP rules had not been issued as of January 2026, and FDA issued draft guidance on records access in January 2026. — [Foley, Mar 2026](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/03/how-mocra-is-reshaping-fda-oversight-of-cosmetics-in-2026/); [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/fda-signals-increased-cosmetics-oversight-through-mocra-implementation-and-other-regulatory-efforts.md); [Wiley alert](https://sitepilot12.firmseek.com/client/wiley2/www/printpilot-publication-Time-Flies-Cosmetic-Manufacturing-Facilities-are-Due-for-FDA-Registration-Renewal.pdf); [EcoMundo](https://ecomundo.eu/en/blog/mocra-deadlines-update-2026); [Pattern (vendor)](https://www.pattern.com/blog/mocra-for-beauty-brands-selling-on-amazon)

**EU**
- **Customs.** €3 per item (or per tariff line) duty on consignments of €150 or less since July 1, 2026, and a €2 per-item Union handling fee expected from November 1, 2026. Product identifiers are reportedly mandatory from November 1, 2026. National fees vary; see Q1. — [VATCalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e3-levy-low-value-e-commerce-import-package-july-2026/); [EUnews](https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/09/03/eu-council-gives-the-green-light-to-customs-reform-from-1-november-a-handling-fee-for-small-parcels/)
- **VAT / IOSS / OSS.** IOSS covers non-EU goods shipped directly to EU consumers in consignments of €150 or less. Consignments above €150 fall outside IOSS, so import VAT and duty are handled at import. Marketplaces such as Amazon, eBay and Etsy are the "deemed supplier" for VAT on low-value imports they facilitate, so the platform's IOSS number is used. Non-EU sellers generally need an EU-established intermediary for IOSS, unless they are established in a country with a VAT mutual-assistance agreement and ship from it. IOSS returns are monthly, and records must be kept for 10 years. OSS applies to intra-EU distance sales above the €10,000 EU-wide threshold, which is relevant for goods dispatched from EU warehouses. Sources disagree about a 2028 "single VAT registration" and Customs Data Hub step. — [Taxually (vendor)](https://www.taxually.com/blog/implementing-ioss-a-step-by-step-guide-for-businesses); [Hellotax (vendor)](https://hellotax.com/blog/ioss-for-non-eu-sellers/); [ShippyPro](https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/ioss-and-oss-explained-eu-vat-rules-for-ecommerce-sellers-in-2026); [Nordlet](https://nordlet.com/en/blog/eu-vat-digital-platforms-oss-ioss-deemed-supplier); [Kud.tax](https://kud.tax/en/blog/oss-and-ioss-2026-differences-vat-thresholds-common-e-commerce-mistakes); [DoDropshipping](https://dodropshipping.com/cross-border-vat-eu-dropshipping/)
- **GPSR.** In force December 13, 2024, per the brief. Dropshipping from non-EU warehouses, or selling private label made outside the EU, requires an EU Responsible Person. Amazon, Etsy, eBay and Shopify-connected channels require GPSR data (responsible person, safety information) before non-EU accounts can list into the EU. A single vendor reports that non-compliant listings are deactivated without warning and take 7–21 days to reactivate. Amazon is variously reported to have begun suspensions in late 2024 or January 2025. One vendor says German, French and Italian market-surveillance authorities are now directly requiring platform takedowns and demanding technical documentation. The sources are compliance-service sellers with an incentive to stress risk, and the figures cited ("32% of listings lack RP", "30–50% sales loss") have no named source. — [SPS Commerce](https://www.spscommerce.com/blog/gpsr-what-eu-sellers-need-to-know-and-implement/); [FlexFulfillment](https://www.flexfulfillment.eu/gpsr-and-eu-product-safety-what-non-eu-brands-must-know-in-2026/); [GetTransport](https://blog.gettransport.com/logistics-guide/gpsr-eu-product-safety-2026-non-eu-seller-guide/); [Taxually](https://www.taxually.com/blog/gpsr-compliance-a-guide-for-amazon-sellers)
- **Withdrawal button / returns.** Directive (EU) 2023/2673 adds Article 11a to the Consumer Rights Directive. From June 19, 2026, online traders, including non-EU businesses targeting EU consumers, must provide a digital "withdrawal function" alongside the existing 14-day right of withdrawal. Withdrawal must be no harder than purchase, and a confirmation must be sent automatically (reportedly by email, not on-screen only). Transposition varies by member state; Germany uses §356a BGB, and Belgium had not transposed at one source's time of writing. Penalties are national. Shopify developers flagged the need for implementation. — [Potomac Law](https://www.potomaclaw.com/news-EUs-New-Withdrawal-Button-Requirement-June-2026-Deadline-Approaching); [Arnold & Porter](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2026/05/eu-withdrawal-button-uk-subscription-rules-and-data-protection-risks-for-us-online-sellers); [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/5/eu-consumer-law-new-withdrawal-button-requirements-for-online-contracts); [Outvio (vendor)](https://outvio.com/blog/eu-withdrawal-button/); [On Tap Group](https://www.ontapgroup.com/blog/eu-withdrawal-button); [Scandiweb](https://scandiweb.com/solutions/eu-withdrawal-button); [Shopify dev community](https://community.shopify.dev/t/new-eu-withdrawal-button-required-by-june-19-2026/35121)

**UK**
- **VAT.** Since 2021, overseas sellers shipping consignments of £135 or less directly to UK consumers must register for UK VAT and charge it at checkout. Where a marketplace facilitates the sale, the marketplace is responsible. For goods already in the UK sold by an overseas seller via a marketplace, the marketplace accounts for VAT at any value. The £135 threshold has never exempted goods from VAT. — [Landmark Global](https://landmarkglobal.com/eu/en/news-insights/uk-vat-on-low-value-goods-the-135-threshold-rule/); [ShippyPro](https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/ecommerce-vat-in-the-uk-2026-registration-thresholds-and-returns)
- **Customs duty.** The £135 relief is to be removed by October 2028 at the latest. The government plans to make sellers or facilitating marketplaces liable for customs duty on low-value imports. Options include a flat rate or normal rates, and a handling fee. Whether the threshold uses intrinsic or customs value is not yet settled. Customs declarations will be required for all imports. — [GOV.UK LVI consultation response (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a511a7b1228eb26a4cab7c6/LVI_Consultation_Response.pdf); [Baker McKenzie](https://globalimportblog.bakermckenzie.com/2026/07/24/uk-low-value-import-relief-to-be-removed-by-october-2028/); [33 Bedford Row](https://33bedfordrow.co.uk/insights/bulletins/the-end-of-the-135-customs-duty-relief-what-importers-and-platforms-need-to-know); [Epic Sourcing UK](https://www.epicsourcing.co.uk/epic-guides/uk-low-value-import-threshold-guide-2026-2028)
- The EU withdrawal-button rule also applies to UK businesses selling into the EU. — [The Lawyer](https://www.thelawyer.com/briefing/the-eus-new-withdrawal-button-is-here-what-uk-businesses-selling-into-the-eu-need-to-know/)

### Inferences
- In the US, CPSC eFiling plus the end of de minimis means children's products (which need a Children's Product Certificate with CPSC-accepted lab testing) and other CPSC-regulated categories now carry document-level friction at every entry. Small dropshippers without a compliant importer of record and test reports should avoid those categories.

- The UK is the friendliest of the three major markets for China direct-ship until 2028: no duty on consignments of £135 or less, with VAT collected at checkout. It is a narrowing window.

## 6. Product characteristics that make good dropshipping products in 2026

### Takeaway
Vendor guidance converges on a few traits: products that are visually demonstrable in short UGC video ("thumb-stop"), solve a specific problem, carry high perceived value supporting a 3x+ markup, and have low return risk. 2026 trade and compliance changes add constraints. Prefer products that are light, compact and higher-value per item (per-item EU fees, duties), sourceable from US/EU stock or non-China origins, and outside heavily regulated categories (children's, cosmetics, supplements, RF electronics, batteries). Consumables or repeat-purchase products help amortise high CAC.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 vendor guides define a "winning product" by its visual "thumb-stop" appeal, its ability to solve a real problem, a high perceived value supporting about 3x markup, and its potential to become a branded asset. Products are increasingly proven through UGC-style demo videos rather than studio ads. — [Dropified (vendor)](https://www.dropified.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-finding-winning-dropshipping-products-in-2026/); [Yakkyofy (vendor)](https://yakkyofy.com/finding-winning-products-for-your-dropshipping-business/); [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/mastering-dropshipping-guide-2026-1793530)
- Novelty products can spike views but "often die in customer support, refunds, and low repeat purchase". USAdrop's five scoring criteria are profit margin, wow factor, problem-solving, visual appeal and return rate. — [Yakkyofy](https://yakkyofy.com/finding-winning-products-for-your-dropshipping-business/); [USAdrop (vendor)](https://usadrop.com/?p=23723)
- Fit and sizing drive about 50–70% of apparel returns, and apparel return rates run 20–40%. — [Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com/learn/ecommerce-return-rates); [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/average-return-rate-by-product-category)
- CJ US/EU warehouse fulfilment requires a minimum of 10 units per variant, and EU stock is "not consistently available across product categories". — [Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/cjdropshipping-vs-zendrop); [Hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/best/european-dropshipping-suppliers)

### Inferences
- **Price band:** per-item fixed costs have risen in both markets: US entry and brokerage charges of about $2.69 per parcel in one example, plus ad-valorem duty; EU €3 + €2 per item. Very cheap impulse items (under about $15–20) shipped from China are now largely unviable. The sweet spot shifts up to about $30–80 per item, or bundles that lift AOV.
- **Weight and size:** small, light, non-fragile products minimise the per-unit cost of US/EU warehouse storage and domestic last mile, and reduce damage returns.
- **Low SKU complexity:** warehouse minimums per variant favour few variants. Avoid size/colour matrices, which also helps with fit-driven returns.
- **Consumables and repeat purchase** offset CAC of about $38+ (platform) to $200+ (fully loaded). However, consumables that are ingested or applied to skin trigger FDA/MoCRA (US) and EU cosmetics regulation. Non-regulated consumables (refills, filters, cleaning or household items) are lower-friction. This is an inference; no source specifically endorsed them.
- **Regulatory filter:** CPSC eFiling (children's and regulated products), MoCRA, FCC and battery rules make "unregulated general merchandise" categories (home organisation, kitchen tools, pet accessories, car accessories, fitness accessories) operationally easier. This is an inference from the compliance findings above.
- **Demonstrability on video** remains core because TikTok Shop and Meta creative drive discovery. TikTok Shop's fast-ship metrics make US-stocked, video-friendly items the strongest fit for that channel.

## 7. Which sourcing countries and regions are growing as dropshipping origins, and for which product types

### Takeaway
Sourcing is shifting from "ship from China" to "import in bulk to the US or EU, then fulfil locally". The goods themselves often remain China-made or China-component. Vietnam is gaining as a "China+1" or final-assembly origin; it faces the same 12.5% new Section 301 tier as China, but without China's legacy 7.5–25% Section 301 stack. Mexico is attractive for US-bound production (10% new Section 301 tier; Printful produces there) but has closed its role as a cross-border parcel conduit for Chinese goods. EU-native suppliers (for example, Spain's BigBuy) and Polish and German warehouses serve EU buyers. Little reliable 2026 evidence exists on Turkey, Eastern Europe beyond Poland, India or Pakistan as dropshipping origins.

### Cited Findings
- The new Section 301 rates from July 24, 2026 are 12.5% for China and Vietnam (plus Thailand and Cambodia) and 10% for India and Mexico. China additionally keeps legacy Section 301 duties. — [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/and-the-tariff-beat-goes-on); [TariffsTool](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/section-301-replacing-section-122); [Jadewell (vendor)](https://jadewellplastic.com/blog/china-vietnam-tariffs-section-301-update-2026)
- **Mexico:**
  - Mexico raised duties on Chinese imports to 33.5% (FreightWaves; FreightAmigo says 35%, effective 2025) and tightened data reporting.

### Inferences
- For US sellers, the tariff gap between Vietnam and China has narrowed under the new Section 301 (both are on the 12.5% tier), but China keeps a legacy 7.5–25% Section 301 premium. Vietnam therefore stays advantaged for apparel, footwear, furniture and home goods, the categories where it has capacity. Mexico (10%) suits heavier or bulkier goods and POD for the US. Transshipment and "assembly to shift origin" strategies carry origin-fraud and forced-labor enforcement risk, given that the new Section 301 tariffs are forced-labor themed.
- For EU sellers, the decisive variable is EU stock rather than country of manufacture, because the €3/€2 charges hit non-EU parcels regardless of origin. EU warehouse availability (Poland and Germany for CJ, Spain for BigBuy) is the practical sourcing map.
- Turkey (textiles, home textiles), Eastern Europe (Poland: furniture and household goods), India (textiles, handicrafts, jewellery) and Pakistan (textiles, leather) are plausible growth origins based on general trade patterns. However, no 2026 source in this research confirmed them as rising dropshipping origins.

### Gaps
- No 2026 data on Turkey, Pakistan, India or Eastern Europe (beyond Polish warehouses) as dropshipping origins.
- No reliable statistics on the share of US or EU dropshipped orders by origin country.
- The country tiers of the July 2026 Section 301 action (about 46 vs. about 60 economies; whether USMCA-qualifying Mexican goods are exempt) need verification against the Federal Register notice (reportedly 91 FR 47318).
