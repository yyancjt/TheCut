## 1. TikTok status (US), TikTok Shop GMV (US/UK/EU/SEA), top and fastest-growing categories/products, and policy changes affecting dropshippers

### Takeaway
TikTok's US business has been run by the TikTok USDS Joint Venture since 22 January 2026. Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold about 15% each and ByteDance keeps 19.9%. ByteDance still owns the algorithm and licenses it to the JV, legal challenges continue, and no shutdown or disruption is in force. TikTok Shop's global GMV is estimated at about $50B in H1 2026 (+92% YoY), with the US at about $11.8B (+103%). The UK is by far the largest European market, the EU-4 (DE/FR/ES/IT) are growing at triple digits from a small base, and Europe is expanding into new countries. Beauty dominates US sales, led by Korean/brand-led products, with womenswear, sports, home and supplements behind it. Fulfillment rules (domestic origin, 2-day dispatch, about 6-day delivery) make classic China-direct dropshipping unworkable on TikTok Shop US.

**Policy changes affecting dropshippers (TikTok Shop US; third-party guides conflict and Seller Center was not verified)**
- **Seller Shipping mandate whiplash:** some guides say TikTok planned to discontinue seller-fulfilled "Seller Shipping" for US sellers (one cites **25 Feb 2026**). Others report that TikTok **paused/reversed** the plan around **17 Feb 2026** after seller pushback, and an Oct 2026 guide still describes Seller Shipping as active. The later sources point to the reversal. — [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/blog/tiktok-shop-reverses-us-shipping-mandate); [AMZ Prep](https://amzprep.com/tiktok-seller-shipping-ends-2026-what-next/); [Jay Group](https://www.jaygroup.com/blog/tiktok-shop-is-ending-independent-shipping/)

Others say compliant dropshipping from US-stocked suppliers is allowed. — [TikTok Shop Profit Calculator guide](https://tiktokshopprofitcalculator.com/guides/dropshipping-guide); [Shoplazza](https://www.shoplazza.com/blog/tiktok-shop-policy-update); [LooperBuy (sourcing vendor)](https://looperbuy.com/blog/tiktok-shop-dropshipping-policy-2026-a-b2b-experts-guide-to-compliant-global-sourcing-with-looperbuy.html)

### Inferences
- The US platform-risk discount on TikTok has largely gone away: the JV has operated for about 10 months with no outage, so brands and sellers are treating TikTok Shop as a durable channel. Residual legal risk centres on the algorithm licence and has not affected commerce operations.
- With Shop-tab search now ~51% of US GMV and LIVE down to ~8%, TikTok Shop increasingly rewards **listing quality, reviews and price** in the way a marketplace does, not only viral video. That favours established sellers with inventory and review history over fly-by-night dropship listings.
- **Classic AliExpress/CJ China-direct dropshipping is effectively incompatible with TikTok Shop US.** Viable models are US-warehouse suppliers, a 3PL with pre-bought inventory, or FBT. Many dropshippers now treat TikTok as **top-of-funnel content** driving to their own Shopify store, where they control shipping promises.
- US top sellers are **brand-led** (medicube, Dr.Melaxin, Toplux), K-beauty and supplements. Unbranded dropshippers cannot easily compete there and face regulatory and liability exposure on supplements and skincare. Higher-AOV, demo-friendly **tools/hardware/household appliances** (for example cordless pressure washers) look like the more realistic opening.
- **Europe is earlier-stage, which can mean less saturation.** The UK is a mature, concentrated market, while DE/FR/ES/IT are growing at triple digits from small bases, with **home improvement** a standout growth vertical across all four. A first-mover advantage may still exist in the EU-4 and the June-2026 expansion countries (NL/BE/PL/AT). Inferred EU shipping/fulfillment requirements are unverified (see Gaps).
- Vendor GMV numbers disagree materially (Charm.io vs FastMoss/Kalodata beauty; SEA H1 consistency). Use these figures for direction and relative rank, not as precise market sizes.

## 2. What went viral in 2025–2026 (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Pinterest), Pinterest Predicts 2026, TikTok Next 2026, Amazon and Google Trends patterns

### Takeaway
The documented 2026 "viral sellers" on social commerce are mostly brand-led beauty/wellness items: medicube body care and PDRN balm, collagen, magnesium. Next come demo-driven home/tool gadgets such as cordless pressure washers and cleaning gadgets. Life cycles are very short. The 2026 trend forecasts from TikTok and Pinterest, both published Dec 2025 using 2024–25 data, emphasise authenticity, purchase verification and "intention over impulse" (TikTok), and sensory/aesthetic niches such as scent layering, jelly/gummy textures, lace, alien-core and maximalist glam (Pinterest). Search-growth tools surface niche breakouts such as taffeta pants, portable laptop monitors and smart bidets.

### Inferences
- The forecasts and the sales data point the same way. TikTok's "intention over impulse/verification hub" framing matches the shift of US TikTok Shop GMV toward the Shop tab and search. Products that survive scrutiny (reviews, real demos, credible brands) win, and gimmicky impulse gadgets are weaker than in 2020–2023.
- Pinterest 2026 trends point to **beauty textures (jelly blush), fragrance layering/decants, lace/nail art, maximalist accessories, boho home decor, and stationery/letter-writing kits**. Most of these are low-ticket, aesthetic-driven goods suited to Pinterest/IG Reels traffic. Because the underlying data is 2024–25, many will be saturated by late 2026.
- The very short life cycle of TikTok viral products (weeks) means dropshippers who chase visible virality are usually late. Search-growth tools show slower, more durable demand, for example portable monitors and smart bidets, which are higher-AOV, problem-solving and AI-search friendly.

## 3. How Amazon (Haul, ads), Temu, Shein, Walmart and Shopify changed the competitive landscape for independent dropshipping stores (US + Europe)

### Takeaway
In the US, the end of de minimis for Chinese goods (May 2025) and tariffs pushed Temu and Shein toward US warehouses and local sellers and weakened their US demand. Amazon Haul now has millions of sub-$10 items and appears in main search, so the low-ticket "cheap gadget" niche is dominated by the giants. Amazon ad revenue is growing faster than its retail sales, which means pay-to-play visibility. Walmart Marketplace is growing about 50% YoY but from a small base. Shopify merchants overall grew GMV about 30% in Q3 2026. In Europe, Temu and Shein have redirected growth investment and an EU de minimis fee is unlikely to slow them, so European dropshippers face rising Chinese-platform competition.

### Cited Findings

**Amazon**
- **Q2 2026 Amazon advertising revenue: $19.8B, +26% YoY** (from $15.7B), against **online stores +15%**. Analysts read this as sellers paying more for visibility; ad revenue also includes Prime Video/display. — [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-q2-2026-earnings/); [BellaVix](https://www.bellavix.com/amazon-q2-2026-amazon-advertising-costs-are-rising-faster-than-seller-revenue/)
- One tracker reports average Amazon PPC CPC **+2% in Q1 2026**. Agency estimates put advertising at ~10–25% of revenue for most sellers, and total fees (including ads) can exceed 50% of revenue for private-label sellers (rough industry estimates). — [Nova Analytics](https://novadata.io/resources/amazon-seller-statistics); [SupplyKick](https://www.supplykick.com/blog/amazon-marketplace-trends-2026)

**Shopify (independent-store ecosystem)**
- **Q3 2026 (quarter ended 30 Sep): GMV $119B (+~29.5–30% YoY), revenue +32% to ~$3.7B**, the sixth straight quarter of ~30% GMV growth. Constant-currency **Europe GMV +33%, North America +28%**. Shop Pay GMV grew >50%. ### Inferences
- **The low-ticket "cheap gadget" lane is closed for paid-ads dropshipping stores.** Temu, Haul (6M+ items under $10, now in main Amazon search) and Shein anchor consumer price expectations, and none of these pay Meta/TikTok CPMs per sale the way a dropshipper does. Independent stores need products where **brand, bundle, education or speed** justify a premium and where AOV is high enough to absorb CPMs.
- The end of US de minimis hits China-direct dropshippers as hard as it hits Temu/Shein, because every parcel is now dutiable. The giants respond with US warehouses, and dropshippers who can access **US-stocked suppliers** gain relative ground. This is the same direction TikTok Shop's domestic-origin rules push.
- **Europe is getting more competitive from Chinese platforms, not less.** Temu and Shein are reallocating growth spending to the EU/UK, so European dropshippers selling generic Chinese goods face rising price competition and ad-auction pressure. The Shopify Europe GMV +33% figure shows independent DTC is also growing fast there.
- Amazon's ad revenue growing ~26% against ~15% retail growth confirms rising pay-to-play costs. For dropshippers this increases the relative appeal of owned channels (email/SMS, creators) and of AI/organic discovery.
- Walmart Marketplace is a growing secondary channel but requires fast, reliable fulfillment (WFS/2-day), so it is not suited to slow-ship dropshipping.

- **Amazon Buy with Prime 2026** changes: no data found.

- Temu/Shein 2026 US demand data may be misdated (see the flag above).

## 6. How AI-driven product discovery (ChatGPT shopping, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, agentic checkout) is changing which stores/products win

### Takeaway
AI shopping is real and growing fast as a traffic source. Adobe forecasts AI referrals to US retail sites up 130% this holiday season, and Shopify reports AI-driven orders up ~3.3x YoY. In-chat checkout has stumbled, though: OpenAI scaled back ChatGPT Instant Checkout in March 2026 after very few merchants went live, and moved to discovery-first. Google is pushing checkout inside AI Mode and Gemini via its Universal Commerce Protocol, built with Shopify, Etsy, Walmart, Target and Wayfair. Most consumers (72%) still don't plan to use AI for holiday shopping. Winners are stores with clean structured product data, reviews and trust signals that AI agents can parse, and these features are largely US-only.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT Instant Checkout** launched **29 Sep 2025** with Etsy, and Shopify merchants were "coming soon". On **24 Mar 2026** OpenAI published "Powering product discovery in ChatGPT", saying the first version "did not offer the level of flexibility" it wanted. Merchants can now use their own checkout while OpenAI focuses on **discovery**. Purchases still happen via retailer apps in ChatGPT (e.g. Instacart, Target). — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/); [Rye](https://rye.com/blog/openai-chatgpt-checkout-agentic-commerce); [UCP Hub](https://ucphub.ai/chatgpt-shopping-checkout-why-openai-scaled-back-and-what-it-means-for-merchants-in-2026/)

Shopify's Harley Finkelstein reportedly said the bottleneck was the AI firms' protocol onboarding. OpenAI has not formally announced discontinuation. — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/); [Rye](https://rye.com/blog/openai-chatgpt-checkout-agentic-commerce); [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/chatgpt-wanted-to-become-the-worlds-biggest-shop)

One source cites **75M+ daily active AI Mode users**. — [Azoma](https://www.azoma.ai/insights/google-i-o-2026-what-the-agentic-commerce-announcements-mean-for-brands); [Ask Phill](https://askphill.com/blogs/blog/agentic-commerce-for-shopify-protocols-platforms-and-what-to-prioritize-in-2026)

### Inferences
- AI assistants recommend products by synthesising reviews, specs, price and merchant reliability. **Thin dropship stores** (copied AliExpress descriptions, no reviews, long or vague shipping times, no return policy) are structurally disadvantaged in AI answers compared with Amazon, Walmart and established DTC brands, and are the stores least likely to be picked by an agent doing checkout.
- Shopify's default Agentic Storefronts give every Shopify store technical eligibility for AI channels. Winning depends on **product-data quality** (GTINs, structured attributes, accurate shipping/returns, real reviews) and on being a **distinct product or brand** an AI can name.
- AI traffic is growing fast from a small base and most consumers aren't yet using it for holiday purchases. For late 2026 it is a **secondary but rising** channel, more important for considered, higher-AOV, spec-driven purchases (e.g. "best portable monitor") than for impulse novelties.
- Europe lags: UCP and agentic checkout are mainly US features, with expansion announced at I/O, so AI-driven discovery is a smaller near-term factor for EU/UK dropshippers. This is inferred from the US-only availability notes.

### Gaps
- **Perplexity shopping** (Buy with Pro, PayPal integration) 2026 status was not researched.
- No hard data on **AI-referral conversion rates** for small or dropship stores.
- Google UCP's **European rollout** status and timing were not confirmed.
- Primary OpenAI and Google announcements were not fetched directly (host fetches blocked). Dates come from CNBC, TechCrunch and Modern Retail coverage.
