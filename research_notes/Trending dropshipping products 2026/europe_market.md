# European (EU + UK) E-commerce and Dropshipping Market, as of November 2026

Research date: 17 November 2026. Method notes: Searches covered 2025-2026 sources. Direct fetches of ecommerce-europe.eu and eurocommerce.eu were blocked by this session's network, so European E-commerce Report figures come from search snippets and the 2025 corrigendum PDF listing, not from a full read of the 2026 report. Many product-trend and ad-cost figures come from vendor or analytics blogs (FastMoss, Kalodata/Lengow, ECDB, ad-benchmark sites). Treat them as directional. Dates are flagged throughout. ## 1. Market size and growth by country (2025-2026); spend per shopper; ad costs vs purchasing power

### Takeaway
European B2C e-commerce grew about 7% in both 2024 and 2025, reaching about EUR 911bn in 2025. Growth is fastest in Eastern Europe (Poland, the Baltics, the Balkans), while large Western markets grow in low single digits (Germany about 4%, UK online values about 6%, France about 7%, mostly from services). Southern Europe and Poland have the cheapest Meta ad inventory (about 30-60% below the UK and Germany). The UK has the highest online share of retail (about 28-29%) and the most expensive ads. A 2026 energy-price shock has pushed eurozone inflation back to about 3-4%, which weighs on consumer confidence.

### Inferences
- Germany and France are large but slow for physical goods. France's +4% for products and Germany's +3.9-4.3% are roughly inflation-level growth, so real growth is close to flat. Growth for a new seller in these markets comes from taking share, not from the market expanding.
- Poland is the clearest growth story among sizable markets: double-digit online growth, low online share of retail (about 9%, so lots of headroom), and the cheapest Meta CPMs. The catch is BLIK/InPost expectations and Allegro's dominance (see Q5/Q6).
- Spain and Italy have about 30-40% cheaper ads than the UK and Germany, while being TikTok Shop's fastest-growing home-goods markets (see Q2). Spain had high inflation (4.9%) in October 2026, so expect price sensitivity.
- The UK has the highest online share, English-language creative, and the biggest TikTok Shop market (forecast EUR 4.89bn GMV 2026), but also the most expensive ads and flat online growth (IMRG 0% forecast). It is a large, saturated, high-CAC market.
- France's falling basket (-3%) and Europe-wide confidence near -18 point to value-seeking. Impulse products in the EUR 15-40 range may convert better than premium ones, but this competes directly with Temu and Shein.

- Full country tables (turnover, growth, e-shopper penetration, spend per e-shopper) from the European E-commerce Report 2026 could not be retrieved, because ecommerce-europe.eu and eurocommerce.eu were blocked in this session. No 2025 figures were obtained for the Netherlands (Thuiswinkel), Spain, Italy, the Nordics, Ireland or Belgium.

- No TikTok Ads or Google Ads CPC benchmarks by EU country were gathered. Meta CPMs are not adjusted for purchasing power.
- The cause of the ECB's 2026 hikes is covered in Q3 (energy shock). The 5 Nov 2026 Bank of England decision was not confirmed.

## 2. Trending categories and products in Europe, 2026

### Takeaway
On TikTok Shop EU, the fastest-growing category in 2026 is Home Improvement (+200-300% in DE/FR/ES/IT). Germany skews to home appliances and utility items (vacuums, strollers), France to kitchen appliances (air fryers), and the UK to beauty (body mists, lip oils, face pads, calcium/collagen balms, teeth-whitening strips). Seasonal spikes in 2026 were extreme for cooling products (UK fan sales up roughly 3,000% week on week in heatwaves) and emergency-preparedness items (radios, torches, power banks after the EU 72-hour guidance). Balcony solar in Germany is maturing (about 430k units in 2025) and units are getting bigger.

### Inferences
- **Strongest product spaces for a small EU/UK seller in 2026-27:**
  1. Home improvement and home gadgets for TikTok-led demand: cleaning tools, organisers, LED and smart lighting, small DIY tools, draft stoppers, and kitchen gadgets in the air-fryer-accessory ecosystem. This is the clearest cross-market growth signal (+200-300%).
  2. Beauty and personal care (UK first): body mists, lip oils, toner pads, teeth-whitening strips, nasal and oral care. These are regulated (EU/UK Cosmetics Regulation responsible person, CPNP/SCPN notification), which raises the barrier but also cuts competition from casual dropshippers.
  3. Energy-cost products, given the 2026 oil and gas shock (Q3): heated throws and blankets, heated clothing, smart plugs and energy monitors, thermostatic radiator valves, window insulation film, dehumidifiers, and balcony-solar accessories (mounts, storage add-ons, monitoring plugs). Larger balcony-solar units suggest demand for batteries and storage add-ons.
  4. Preparedness kits (EU 72-hour guidance, Nov 2026): crank and solar radios, torches and headlamps, power banks, water containers and filters, first-aid kits, document pouches. Demand is likely news-driven and spiky. It peaked around the Nov 2026 guidance and is likely strongest in the Nordics, Poland, the Baltics and Germany (inference).
  5. Seasonal cooling (May-August): handheld and neck fans, cooling towels, portable evaporative coolers. Spikes are extreme but short (days to weeks) and stock runs out. A pre-stocked EU or UK warehouse is needed to catch them; China dropshipping lead times miss the window.
- Q4 gifting (advent calendars, beauty gift sets) was already trending by mid-September in the UK.
- Mechanics matter more than product: on TikTok Shop EU, affiliate-creator video drives most sales, so sellers need affiliate programmes and sample budgets.

## 3. European events shaping demand (energy, war and preparedness, climate, cost of living, rates, elections)

### Takeaway
The dominant 2026 macro event is a new energy shock. The war involving Iran, following US-Israeli strikes in late February 2026, nearly closed the Strait of Hormuz. That pushed euro-area energy inflation to about +14% and headline inflation from 1.9% (Feb) to about 3.3-3.8% (Aug-Sept), prompting an ECB hike to a 2.50% deposit rate in September. On top of the legacy of the 2022 crisis, this revives demand for energy-saving and heating products. Separately, the EU's 72-hour preparedness push (strategy March 2025; detailed population guidelines early November 2026) and repeated summer heatwaves create spiky, news-driven demand.

### Cited Findings
**2026 energy shock and inflation**
- Euro-area inflation was 1.9% in February 2026, with energy falling 3.1% year on year. It rose to 2.5% in March "amid Iran war", 3.0% in April (highest since Sept 2023), 2.9% in July and 3.3% in August (flash; final 3.2%). Energy was +14.3% year on year in August (July: +10.3%). Fuels and heating oil were hit hardest. Core inflation was 2.4% — [Euronews, 1 Sept 2026](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/01/eurozone-inflation-jumps-to-33-in-august-as-energy-prices-surge); [Euronews, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.euronews.com/2026/03/31/eurozone-inflation-jumps-to-25-amid-iran-war-will-the-ecb-hike-rates); [Irish Times, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/03/03/euro-zone-inflation-unexpectedly-rises-to-19-in-february/); [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/euro-area/inflation-cpi/news/552268)
- Cause: oil and gas price surge from the war in Iran, with disruption to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Reuters-based reporting says US-Israeli strikes in late February led to an almost complete closure of Hormuz and attacks on Gulf energy infrastructure — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/01/eurozone-inflation-jumps-to-33-in-august-as-energy-prices-surge); [Jakarta Post (Reuters)](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2026/03/31/euro-zone-inflation-surges-past-ecb-target-on-oil-shock)
- September 2026 preliminary inflation was 3.8% (aggregator; also cited in a Danish ECB report headline) — [ROIC News](https://www.roic.ai/news/eurozone-inflation-jumps-to-38-above-forecasts-10-02-2026); [Lovguiden](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/den-europaeiske-centralbank/2026-10-29-ecb-holder-renten-uaendret-paa-250-trods-inflation-paa-38)
- Spain and Italy cut taxes to cushion energy prices. The ECB projects 3.0% inflation for 2026, 2.5% for 2027 and 2.1% for 2028 — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/01/eurozone-inflation-jumps-to-33-in-august-as-energy-prices-surge)
- The ECB hiked to a 2.50% deposit rate (Sept 2026) and held on 29 Oct 2026. ## 4. Temu, Shein, AliExpress, TikTok Shop: performance and regulation; China-direct dropshipping vs EU fulfilment

### Takeaway
The economics of China-direct, low-value parcels into the EU changed sharply in 2026. A flat EUR 3 customs duty per item category applies to parcels under EUR 150 from 1 July 2026, and an EU EUR 2 handling fee per item applies from 1 November 2026. Together they add about EUR 5 or more per order line, roughly 15-25% on a EUR 20-30 item. Temu was fined EUR 200M under the DSA (May 2026), Shein is under formal DSA proceedings, and France's ultra-fast-fashion law adds per-item eco-penalties of up to EUR 12 (from Sept 2026). The UK keeps its GBP 135 relief until March 2029 at the latest. Temu and Shein are responding by moving stock into EU warehouses. For small sellers, EU or UK-warehoused fulfilment is now the default; China-direct only works for higher-ticket items.

### Cited Findings
**EU customs reform**
- The EUR 150 duty exemption ended, and a flat EUR 3 duty per item category on low-value parcels has applied since 1 July 2026. It is temporary until about July 2028, when the EU Customs Data Hub and standard duties take over — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/eu-ends-eur150-duty-exemption-charging-shein-and-temu-eur3-per-item/); [E-Commerce Institute Cologne](https://ecommerceinstitut.de/eu-package-fee-from-1-july-2026-what-the-new-customs-rules-mean-for-temu-shein-and-european-e-commerce/); [To Vima](https://www.tovima.com/finance/eu-to-charge-e3-fee-on-shein-temu-parcels-from-july-2026/); [Tech Times, 2 Jul 2026, "5.9 billion imports now taxed"](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319496/20260702/eu-customs-duty-hits-shein-temu-parcels-59-billion-imports-now-taxed.htm)
- An EU-wide EUR 2 "Union handling fee" per item, set by a Commission delegated act, applies from 1 November 2026, on top of the EUR 3 duty. National handling fees are supposed to be dropped once it applies. Some secondary sources still describe the November date as "no later than", and one headline mentions a rise to EUR 5 per parcel without detail — [The European Operator](https://theeuropeanoperator.com/p/it-s-official-the-eu-s-2-handling-fee-is-set-and-it-starts-in-november); [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/18538/eu-handling-fee-2-euro-non-eu-parcels); [l0g.fr](https://l0g.fr/en/analysis/small-parcels-fees/); [Eurotax](https://www.eurotax.fr/en/2028-customs-reform-and-the-eus-e2-parcel-handling-fee/)
- National fees: France's national EUR 2 small-parcel tax was reported as repealed or suspended (effective 1 July 2026), and no French fee is currently active. Italy's national fee is unsettled (start dates of 1 Oct, 1 Nov or 1 Dec 2026 have been reported, and it may not apply once the EU fee starts) — [SimplyVAT](https://simplyvat.com/blog/small-parcel-levy/); [Passport](https://passportglobal.com/blog/eu-uk-customs-developments/); [Shippo](https://goshippo.com/blog/new-eu-import-fees-in-2026)
- Workaround: Shein and Temu reportedly moved a significant share of EU-bound inventory into French, German and Polish 3PLs before 1 July, so orders ship domestically and avoid the flat duty (single secondary source) — [Novadata](https://novadata.io/resources/news/shein-temu-eu-3-euro-duty-eu-inventory-workaround-july-2026); [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/eu-parcel-fee-de-minimis-shein-temu-cross-border-dtc)

**DSA enforcement**
- On 28 May 2026 the Commission fined Temu EUR 200M (about $232M) under the DSA for failing to diligently assess systemic risks of illegal products. The case also covered recommender systems and the influencer affiliate programme. Proceedings opened 31 Oct 2024 and preliminary findings came in July 2025. It is the largest DSA fine so far (the second ever, after X's EUR 120M in Dec 2025). Temu calls it disproportionate. An action plan was due by 28 Aug 2026 or periodic penalties may follow — [EC press release IP/26/1178](https://europa.eu/newsroom/ecpc-failover/pdf/ip-26-1178_en.pdf); [Tech Policy Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/eu-tests-limits-of-platform-risk-assessments-with-200-million-temu-fine); [Khaleej Times (Reuters)](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/temu-fined-232-million-for-breaching-eu-rules-on-sale-of-illegal-products-2); [Slaughter and May](https://thelens.slaughterandmay.com/post/102n15m/european-commission-fines-temu-200-million-under-the-dsa)
- Shein: the Commission opened formal DSA proceedings (reported as February 2026) over illegal products, addictive design and recommender-system transparency. No decision had been found as of Nov 2026 — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/the-european-commission-fines-temu-eur-200-mln-over-dsa-risk-assessment-failures) (attribution from search summary; verify)

**France: anti-ultra-fast-fashion law**
- Loi n° 2026-602: final vote 29 June 2026, promulgated 8 July, published 9 July 2026. It targets "ultra-fast fashion" without naming companies — [Fashion Law Journal](https://fashionlawjournal.com/france-anti-fast-fashion-law-2026-explained/)
- Since September 2026, France levies per-item eco-penalties from EUR 0.25 (socks or boxers) to EUR 12 (coat), capped at 50% of the pre-tax price. They are calculated from products sold, prices and repairability, and will increase from 2030. Advertising and influencer promotion are restricted (one source dates the ad ban to 2027; unconfirmed). Officials say Zara and H&M are not expected to be affected. China's commerce ministry called the law discriminatory and possibly in breach of WTO rules — [RTE (Reuters), 1 Sept 2026](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0901/1589897-france-targets-shein-and-temu-with-fast-fashion-fines/); [Business of Fashion](https://businessoffashion-businessoffashion-prod.web.arc-cdn.net/news/retail/france-targets-shein-and-temu-with-fast-fashion-fines/)
- French authorities reportedly fined Shein over EUR 22M in June 2026 (traceability, environmental labelling, delivery times), after EUR 40M in 2025 for misleading discounts (single secondary source; verify) — [Strasbourg Centre](https://strasbourgcentre.com/frances-ultra-fast-fashion-crackdown-what-the-shein-and-temu-penalties-mean-for-europe/)
- The end of duty-free access for cheap parcels in the EU and US hit Shein's valuation ahead of its Hong Kong listing — [RTE (Reuters)](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0901/1589897-france-targets-shein-and-temu-with-fast-fashion-fines/)

**Platform scale**
- Temu and Shein have about EUR 4.7bn in German sales (HDE 2026) — [retail-news.de](https://retail-news.de/hde-online-monitor-2026-onlinehandel/)
- TikTok Shop: more than 100k businesses joined across FR/DE/IE/IT/ES, and daily GMV grew at triple-digit rates from Aug 2025 to Feb 2026 — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/tiktok-shop-europe-expansion-cross-border-sellers)
- TikTok Shop reached 15th among German online retailers and 16th in Spain within about 18 months (as reported in 2026 trade coverage) — [EuroShop mag](https://www.euroshop-tradefair.com/en/media-news/euroshopmag/retail-marketing/tiktok-shop-establishes-itself-in-european-retail-in-2026-numbers-and-retailer-strategies); [Cross-Border Commerce Association](https://www.cross-border-association.com/tiktok-shop-nears-e500m-in-europe/)

**UK**
- Autumn Budget 2025: the GBP 135 low-value import (LVI) customs relief will be removed, and LVIs made subject to tariffs, from March 2029 at the latest. The consultation ran 26 Nov 2025 to 6 Mar 2026. The Times reported ministers considering faster action (unconfirmed). ICAEW says 2029 is too late — [Deloitte Taxscape](https://taxscape.deloitte.com/measures-autumn-budget-2025/customs-duty-relief-for-low-value-imports.aspx); [DWF](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/1/reform-of-customs-treatment); [ICAEW, Mar 2026](https://www.icaew.com/insights/tax-news/2026/mar-2026/government-should-act-sooner-on-low-value-imports-says-icaew); [GOV.UK consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/reforming-the-customs-treatment-of-low-value-imports-into-the-united-kingdom/low-value-import-reform-consultation-launch-event-transcript)

**GPSR (EU General Product Safety Regulation)**
- Article 16 requires an EU-established economic operator (the "responsible person") for every consumer product sold in the EU. Dropshippers are not exempt. Article 19 requires listings to show the manufacturer's details and, for non-EU manufacturers, the EU responsible person's details — [Complir GPSR guide](https://www.complir.io/guides/gpsr-regulation-guide); [Spocket (ES)](https://www.spocket.co/es/blogs/cumplimiento-de-la-normativa-de-seguridad-de-productos-de-la-ue); [GetTransport](https://blog.gettransport.com/sv/logistics-guide/gpsr-eu-product-safety-2026-non-eu-seller-guide/)
- Marketplaces enforce GPSR: Amazon suspends listings without a valid EU responsible person, bol.com took listings without GPSR manufacturer data offline from 1 April 2026, and Kaufland withholds offers without an EU responsible person. Market surveillance authorities increasingly ask for the underlying documentation — [WisePIM EU compliance tracker](https://wisepim.com/reports/eu-product-compliance-tracker); [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/ecommerce/news/eu-product-safety-laws-reach-sellers-4f8196b3-5ee3-450d-a8cf-1854a76b9969/)

### Inferences
- **China-direct dropshipping into the EU is structurally weakened.** A typical single-item order now carries about EUR 5 in extra duty and handling, plus VAT under IOSS. On a EUR 20-30 AOV this wipes out much of the margin, and longer transit hurts conversion against Temu and Shein, which are themselves moving to EU warehouses with 2-5 day delivery. A small seller should:
  - Use EU 3PL or print-on-demand fulfilment (Poland, Germany, the Netherlands, Czechia) for core SKUs, bringing stock in by sea or rail in B2B consignments, where the duty and handling-fee mechanics differ from B2C parcels.
  - Keep China-direct only for high-AOV items (EUR 60+) where EUR 5 is a small share, or for test SKUs.
  - Bundle multiple units of the same item category into one parcel. Because the duty is per item category, a bundle pays EUR 3 once (inference from the "per category" design; verify with a customs broker).
- **The UK is the last big European market with China-direct parcel economics intact** (GBP 135 relief until 2029 at the latest; import VAT is still collected at the point of sale). That makes the UK the most dropshipping-friendly market through 2026-28, but the policy window could close early.
- **Fashion into France is high-risk** for anything resembling ultra-fast fashion: eco-penalties, influencer-ad restrictions, and aggressive DGCCRF enforcement (Shein fines).
- **GPSR is the practical gatekeeper.** Without an EU responsible person and compliant listings, marketplaces (Amazon, bol, Kaufland, likely Allegro and TikTok Shop) will delist products. DTC stores are exposed to market-surveillance and customs checks.
- **Enforcement will hit platform sellers.** DSA pressure on Temu (illegal products, affiliate programme) pushes platforms to tighten seller vetting, which raises the bar for small cross-border sellers on Temu, Shein and TikTok Shop marketplaces.

### Gaps
- The exact legal text and amount of the 1 Nov 2026 EU handling fee (per item vs per parcel, and whether it also applies to goods from EU warehouses or only non-EU direct consignments) should be confirmed in the Official Journal or on the Commission customs (TAXUD) pages, which were not fetched. Secondary sources conflict.
- No 2026 data on AliExpress's EU performance or regulation was found in this session.
- No Shein or Temu UK 2026 sales figures were found.
- The status of the EU-level fast-fashion and textile measures (Waste Framework Directive textile EPR) and of DSA cases against AliExpress was not checked.
- Switzerland (VAT on low-value imports) and Norway (VOEC scheme, NOK 3,000 threshold) were not researched this session. Both sit outside the EU customs reform and need separate VAT registration (background knowledge, unverified).

## 5. European consumer preferences vs the US: sustainability, Right to Repair, delivery, payments, localisation, returns

### Takeaway
European shoppers expect local payment methods (iDEAL about 70% in the Netherlands, BLIK 60-70% in Poland, Bancontact in Belgium, PayPal and invoice/BNPL in Germany and Austria, Klarna across the Nordics and DACH), locker and pickup-point delivery (InPost in Poland, PostNL, DHL Packstation), full localisation (language, currency, local customer service) and a legal 14-day no-reason withdrawal right. Sustainability and repairability are increasingly written into law (France's repairability-based eco-penalties, the EU Right to Repair Directive, GPSR traceability), not just preferred. Marketplaces dominate more than in the US (56.7% of German online sales).

### Cited Findings
- **Netherlands:** iDEAL holds about 70% of e-commerce transactions. Wero (the EPI wallet) began rolling out in early 2026, with iDEAL migration targeted by 31 Dec 2027 — [Conversion Club](https://conversion.club/playbook/which-payment-methods-to-offer-in-each-country/); [Akurateco](https://akurateco.com/blog/payment-methods-by-country)
- **Poland:** BLIK dominates (about 71% of transactions in one benchmark, 62% in another). InPost Paczkomaty parcel lockers are a standard delivery option — [ShopRank](https://shoprank.com/); [Conversion Club](https://conversion.club/playbook/which-payment-methods-to-offer-in-each-country/)
- **Belgium:** Bancontact is the domestic payment brand. **Austria:** EPS, Klarna, PayPal. **Germany:** PayPal, invoice-style BNPL and direct debit (PayPal share figures conflict: 57% vs 29%). Sofort was folded into Klarna in 2024 — [Aldgate Pay](https://aldgatepay.com/resources/local-payment-methods-europe/); [Payfasto](https://payfasto.com/local-payment-methods-europe/); [Senorit expansion guide](https://senorit.de/en/blog/ecommerce-europe-expansion-guide-2025)
- **Marketplaces dominate Germany:** 56.7% of online sales in 2025 — [e-commerce-magazin.de](https://www.e-commerce-magazin.de/hde-online-monitor-2026-ecommerce-bleibt-einzelhandels-wachstumsmotor-a-63a7fcb00764be21248583da3cb54e15/)
- **Price-constrained French consumer:** average basket -3% in 2025, transactions +10% ("fragmented consumption under price constraint") — [Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/bilan-ecommerce-2025-196-milliards-baisse-panier-moyen/)
- **Sustainability in law:** France's fast-fashion penalties include repairability in the formula. Shein was reportedly fined in France for environmental-labelling failures — [RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0901/1589897-france-targets-shein-and-temu-with-fast-fashion-fines/); [Strasbourg Centre](https://strasbourgcentre.com/frances-ultra-fast-fashion-crackdown-what-the-shein-and-temu-penalties-mean-for-europe/)
- **E-waste awareness:** UK charity Material Focus raised concerns over about 8M disposable mini fans in 2026 — [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/britons-buy-8m-mini-fans-060022000.html)
- **TikTok-native buying:** 63.8% of TikTok Shop EU sales come from in-feed video and 17.2% from live. Livestream shopping is much less central than in Asia — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/tiktok-shop-europe-q2-2026-e500m-across-four-markets)
- Various vendor sources note the EU's 14-day mandatory withdrawal right, in contrast with voluntary US return policies — [Senorit](https://senorit.de/en/blog/ecommerce-europe-expansion-guide-2025)

### Inferences
- **Payments checklist by market:** NL needs iDEAL (Wero later). BE needs Bancontact. PL needs BLIK (and Przelewy24). DE/AT need PayPal plus Klarna (invoice) and SEPA debit. Nordics need Klarna, Vipps/MobilePay and Swish (background knowledge). UK needs cards, Apple Pay, PayPal and Klarna/Clearpay. Shopify Payments, Stripe, Mollie and Adyen cover most of these.
- **Delivery:** offering locker or pickup-point delivery (InPost, PostNL, DHL Packstation, Mondial Relay in France, Vinted Go) is close to mandatory in PL, NL, FR and the Nordics, and expected delivery times are 1-3 days domestically. This again favours EU warehousing over China-direct.
- **Returns:** the 14-day statutory withdrawal right (plus 14 days to refund, and the seller bears the risk if information duties are not met) and Germany's high return culture, especially for fashion, mean margin models must include returns. Fashion and sizing items carry more risk than hard goods.
- **Localisation:** native-language storefronts, local customer service, EUR or PLN pricing including VAT, local legal pages (Impressum in DE, CGV in FR) and correct withdrawal-form wording are table stakes. Machine translation alone is a known conversion killer in DE and FR (common practitioner view; not sourced here).
- **Sustainability claims:** under incoming EU rules on green claims and empowering consumers for the green transition (background knowledge: Directive 2024/825 applies from Sept 2026), vague "eco-friendly" claims are risky. Durable, repairable, energy-saving positioning is safer if it can be substantiated.

### Gaps

- No primary-source data on European vs US sustainability willingness-to-pay was gathered.
- Directive 2024/825 (empowering consumers, green claims) application date and the status of the Green Claims Directive were not verified in this session.

## 6. Best entry countries and regions for a new small seller

### Takeaway
No single best market exists; the trade-off is between size/AOV and ad cost/competition. For a new small DTC or dropshipping seller in late 2026, the strongest options are: (1) the UK for English-language testing and the only big market where China-direct parcels remain viable until 2029 (but it has the highest CPMs); (2) Spain and Italy for cheap ads and the fastest TikTok Shop home-category growth; (3) Poland for double-digit growth and the lowest CPMs (with BLIK, InPost and Allegro requirements); (4) Germany and the Netherlands as larger, higher-trust markets once there is EU stock, GPSR compliance and localisation. France is large but the most regulated for fashion.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **A suggested phased entry for a small seller:**
  1. **Test in the UK** (English creative, TikTok Shop UK maturity, China-direct still viable). Watch for the 2029 cliff or an earlier change, and budget for high CAC.
  2. **First EU market: Spain and/or Ireland**, or **the Netherlands** for early TikTok Shop mover advantage. Spain has cheaper ads and the strongest home-improvement growth. Ireland gives an English-language EU test (small but high-income). The Netherlands has high digital maturity and a new TikTok Shop, but iDEAL is mandatory.
  3. **Scale to Germany** once there is EU warehouse stock (Poland or Germany 3PL), a GPSR responsible person, a German storefront, PayPal and Klarna invoice, and DHL/Packstation delivery. It has the largest physical-goods TikTok Shop market and strong home and appliance demand.
  4. **Poland** as a cost-efficient growth market, especially if fulfilling from a Polish 3PL (which also serves DE and CZ cheaply). It requires Polish localisation, BLIK and InPost, and competing with Allegro and Temu's Polish warehouses.
  5. **France:** good for home and kitchen (air-fryer ecosystem), but avoid low-price fashion. Expect strict consumer-law enforcement (DGCCRF).
- **The Nordics** (high income, English widely spoken, high preparedness interest) were not deeply researched this session. Higher CPMs and strong local retailers likely make them second-wave markets (inference).
- **Switzerland and Norway** sit outside the EU customs union and need separate VAT handling. They are high-AOV but adding complexity; treat them as later expansions (background knowledge).
