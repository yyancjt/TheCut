# Storage, fulfillment and shipping costs for small dropshipping / DTC sellers, US + Europe (EU + UK), late 2026

**Method and source caveats**
- Research date: 17 Nov 2026.
- In this session WebFetch could not reach almost any host: pirateship.com, usps.com, 3plcenter.com, cjdropshipping.com and getbyrd.com all failed DNS. Every figure below therefore comes from search-engine excerpts of the cited pages, not from a full reading. Where the excerpt tied a number to one page among several, I flag the attribution as uncertain.
- Most 3PL and supplier figures come from vendor or affiliate blogs with a commercial interest, or from third-party reviews. Almost no US or EU 3PL publishes a 2026 rate card: ShipBob, ShipMonk, Red Stag, Byrd, Huboo, Zendbox and Bigblue are all quote-based. Treat these numbers as "starting-from" or benchmark figures.
- **FX used only in worked examples:** 1 EUR ≈ 1.15 USD and 1 USD ≈ 7.2 CNY. These are my own planning assumptions, not sourced rates.
- **Fee-owner labels used throughout:**
  - **[SUPPLIER]** = supplier or dropship platform.
  - **[3PL]** = warehouse.
  - **[CARRIER]** = postal service, courier or freight forwarder.
  - **[CUSTOMS]** = duties, government fees and broker charges.

## 1. Pure dropshipping (no storage): what suppliers charge to ship China→US and China→EU/UK in 2026, and what duty, fees and brokerage add per parcel

### Takeaway
China-direct parcels now carry fixed per-parcel government costs in both markets.
- **US:** full ad-valorem duty on every parcel (de minimis is suspended), plus at least a $2.77 MPF per informal entry, plus a clearance or DDP service margin.
- **EU (since 1 Nov 2026):** €3 flat duty per tariff category plus a €2 handling fee per item, plus import VAT via IOSS.
- **UK:** still duty-free at £135 or less, with VAT collected at checkout.

International freight for light parcels appears to be roughly $4–8 for 500 g and $8–20 for 1 kg, but no 2026 rate card from CJ, YunExpress, 4PX or AliExpress was retrievable. The per-weight table under Inferences is my own estimate.

**US duties and fees per parcel [CUSTOMS]**
- **De minimis status:** suspended for all origins since 29 Aug 2025 (China and Hong Kong since 2 May 2025). From 28 Feb 2026, postal shipments may use only the ad-valorem duty method; the $80–200 flat per-item option ended. A new postal informal entry process applies. — [UPS tariffs page](https://www.ups.com/vn/en/shipping/international-shipping/tariffs); [Federal Register 2026-12669](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry)
- **Duty stack on China-origin goods (since 24 Jul 2026):**
  - Legacy Section 301: 25% on Lists 1–3, 7.5% on List 4A.
  - New "forced-labor" Section 301 of 12.5%, stacked on top.
  - Plus the MFN rate.
  - — [Global Trade Alert](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/forced-labour-section-301-final-action); [Customs-Lookup China duty stack](https://www.customs-lookup.com/tariff-changes/china-import-duty-stack/)
- **Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF):**
  - Informal entry: **$2.77 per entry** for FY2027, effective 1 Oct 2026, up from $2.69.
  - Formal entry: 0.3464% ad valorem, with a minimum of **$34.58** and a maximum of **$670.86** for FY2027 (FY2026: $33.58 / $651.50).
  - — [Livingston](https://www.livingstonintl.com/cbp-to-increase-user-fees-for-fiscal-year-2027-effective-october-1-2026/); [Junologistics](https://www.junologistics.com/customs-updates/us-customs-user-fees-to-increase-for-fiscal-year-2027); [Mohawk Global](https://mohawkglobal.com/trade-translation/cbp-adjusts-customs-user-fees-for-fiscal-year-2027-10-1-2026/); [CBP CSMS bulletin (FY2026)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/3eb24a9?reqfrom=share)
- **Courier fees when the consignee is billed (UPS):**
  - Disbursement fee on US-bound shipments: **2% of duty/tax, minimum $14.00**.
  - **$3 per line** when more than three entry lines are entered.
  - Informal entry covers shipments up to $2,500; formal entry applies above $2,500.
  - — [UPS tariffs & international shipping](https://www.ups.com/vn/en/shipping/international-shipping/tariffs)
- **Courier entry and advancement fees** can add **$15–$40+** to a low-value import. One customs-software vendor's example shows about a $15 courier broker fee on a $30 item. Both are secondary, vendor sources. — [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/the-new-cost-of-small-parcels-after-de-minimis/); [Tandom](https://tandom.ai/resources/postal-vs-courier-duty-rules-dtc)

**EU duties and fees per parcel [CUSTOMS]**
- **€3 flat customs duty** on parcels under €150, since 1 Jul 2026, temporary until about 1 Jul 2028.
  - It is charged per tariff category (HS6) in the consignment, not per parcel: three different product categories cost €9.
  - Some sources say "per item".
  - — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/eu-ends-eur150-duty-exemption-charging-shein-and-temu-eur3-per-item/); [Carraglobe](https://carraglobe.com/?p=5557)
- **€2 handling fee** per item on B2C distance-sale imports, applied from **1 Nov 2026** at any value. It is non-refundable and stacks on the €3. The Council of the EU gave final approval in Sept 2026. — [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-action-influx-of-small-parcels/); [Hellotax](https://hellotax.com/blog/eu-3-euro-customs-duty-2-euro-handling-fee/); [Amazon Supply Chain blog](https://supplychain.amazon.co.uk/blog/eucustoms-deminimis)
- **UK:** consignments of £135 or less stay customs-duty-free until the relief is replaced, **by Oct 2028 at the latest** (confirmed 13 Jul 2026). Duty will then be paid quarterly after entry. — [GOV.UK: Reforming customs rules for low value imports](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reforming-customs-rules-for-low-value-imports); [Meridian Global Services](https://meridianglobalservices.com/uk-to-abolish-135-low-value-import-relief-by-october-2028/)

### Inferences
**Estimated supplier/line freight per parcel in late 2026, excluding duty.** This is my own interpolation from the ranges above. The registration (per-parcel) fee dominates at low weights.

| Weight | China→US (tariff-incl./DDP small-parcel line) | China→EU (DE/FR) | China→UK |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200 g | ~$4–6 | ~€4–6 | ~£3.5–5 |
| 500 g | $4–8 (cited) | ~€5–9 | ~£4.5–7 |
| 1 kg | $8–15 (cited) | $10–20 (cited) ≈ €9–17 | $10–20 (cited) ≈ £8–16 |

**Per-parcel customs add-ons [CUSTOMS], on top of freight:**
- **US:**
  - Duty = value × (MFN + 7.5% or 25% + 12.5%).
  - On an $8 item that is about $1.90–3.30 (assuming a 4% MFN).
  - Plus $2.77 MPF per informal entry. It is not confirmed that MPF is assessed on *postal* informal entries.
  - Plus whatever clearance margin the DDP line embeds. I found no published per-parcel figure for that margin.
  - If a courier (UPS) bills the consignee DAP-style, the **$14 minimum disbursement fee** alone exceeds the value of most dropship items. DDP is effectively mandatory.
- **EU:** €3 per tariff category plus €2 per item = **€5 minimum per single-item order**, plus 19–21% import VAT under IOSS.
  - A 3-SKU, 3-item order carries about €15 in fixed charges before VAT.
- **UK:** £0 duty at £135 or less. VAT is collected at checkout by the seller or marketplace and is a sales tax, not a logistics cost.
  - **The UK is the only one of the three markets where the China-direct per-parcel cost structure is essentially unchanged.**
- **Claims to discount:** some blogs claim a "$150–350 customs broker fee on each dropshipped order". That figure describes formal entries (over $2,500), not low-value informal parcels, and should be disregarded. This is my inference from the UPS informal/formal threshold above.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 rate cards were found for CJPacket, CJ US/EU lines, YunExpress, 4PX, Yanwen or AliExpress Choice. All per-weight figures are estimates from secondary sources, several of them dated.
- The per-parcel clearance or brokerage margin that consolidated DDP lines charge for US informal entries was not published anywhere found.
- It is unconfirmed whether the $2.77 MPF applies to the new postal informal entries.
- There are no reliable 2026 transit-time statistics for China→US postal channels.
- No source said how AliExpress or CJ split the €3 and €2 fees between product price, shipping line and checkout.

## 2. Supplier warehouse programs (CJdropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket/AutoDS, BigBuy): storage, free periods, minimum stock, inbound fees and domestic shipping per order

### Takeaway
Supplier warehouses are the cheapest way into US or EU stock for small sellers.
- **Storage:** typically free for 30–90 days, then about $0.63–0.80 per m³ per day at CJ. Zendrop advertises no storage or receiving fees.
- **Catches:** minimum stock (CJ: 10 units per variant), limited US/EU catalogue coverage, and opaque per-order shipping, which is quoted in-app rather than published.
- No supplier publishes a 2026 per-order domestic shipping rate card. The only figures found are illustrative examples.

### Inferences
- **CJ storage is cheap compared with 3PLs:** $0.63 per m³ per day ≈ **$19 per m³ per month**. German 3PLs charge €28–42 per m³ per month (section 4). The real costs of the supplier-warehouse model are instead:
  - The 10-unit-per-variant minimum, which ties up capital.
  - Higher unit prices that embed inbound freight and duty.
  - No control over the pick/pack service level.
- **Where duty sits:** in supplier-warehouse models the supplier's US or EU entity is usually importer of record. Duty and inbound freight are therefore buried in the product price, not shown as separate fees. Sellers should compare *landed* unit price plus per-order shipping against China-direct.
- **Rough per-order logistics:** for a light item from a supplier US warehouse, about **$5–6 per order** (Zendrop's $5.20 example) plus the embedded inbound and duty uplift in the product price. From CJ's EU warehouse, about **€4 per order** (CJ's own example).

### Gaps
- No Spocket warehouse or storage fees were found for 2026.
- There are no official CJ or Zendrop per-order domestic shipping rate cards. CJ and Zendrop receiving fees for private inventory were not found.
- The CJ free-storage period is unresolved: 30, 60 or 90 days.
- The BigBuy per-order handling fee and Spain-to-EU shipping rates were not found.

## 3. US 3PL pricing in 2026 (ShipBob, ShipMonk, Red Stag, Flexport, Amazon MCF, budget 3PLs)

### Takeaway
For a small US seller, expect:
- **Pick & pack:** about **$2.50–3.50 per order** (survey average $3.20 B2C).
- **Storage:** about **$5 per bin, $10 per shelf, $20–40 per pallet** per month, or **$0.45–0.75 per cubic foot**.
- **Receiving:** about **$10 per pallet** or **$35–45 per hour**.
- **Monthly minimums:** about **$250–275** at ShipBob and ShipMonk.

Final invoices typically run 20–50% above the headline rate once extras are added. Amazon MCF raised single-unit fees by about $0.35–0.41 in Jan 2026 and added a 3.5% surcharge plus peak fees.

### Inferences
- **The duty rate is the same either way.** Bulk import does not lower US duty compared with parcels. What it saves is per-parcel MPF ($2.77) and DDP clearance margins, spread instead over a single formal entry (about $300–700 of fixed costs: broker, ISF, MPF minimum, bond share, drayage).

  - Duty is assessed on the B2B transaction value, which is usually lower than a retail-ish declared parcel value. This is my inference; no source compared parcel versus bulk declared values.
- **EU bulk import** avoids the €3 + €2 low-value charges entirely. It pays the conventional tariff for the HS code, plus import VAT that a VAT-registered importer can normally deduct as input VAT. This is standard EU VAT mechanics, not verified in this session.
  - **Destination fixed fees are the dominant per-unit cost for small EU LCL shipments:** about €300–600 for deconsolidation, clearance and delivery.
- **Air versus LCL for a 150 kg / 0.75 cbm shipment:**
  - Air at about $6 per kg ≈ $900 freight plus clearance.
  - LCL ≈ $150–300 minimum freight plus fixed fees.
  - LCL saves about $400–600 but adds 4–6 weeks of transit and inventory float.

## 7. Returns: cost to process a return in the US vs EU (14-day withdrawal right)

### Takeaway
- **US 3PL return processing:** about **$3–6 per return** (2026 survey average **$3.56**, down from $4.06), plus **$2–5** for inspection and grading. A return label costs about the same as an outbound USPS label (~$7.50–8.50 for 1 lb zone 4).
- **EU and UK:** processing benchmarks are lower in Poland (labelling/returns €0.50–2.00) and higher in Germany. A domestic DHL, PostNL or Royal Mail return label is about €3.20–7.50 or about £3.60.
- **China-direct dropshipping:** returning goods to China is uneconomic, so returns are usually refunded without return or returned to a local address. That turns the product cost into the return cost.

  - **UK:** about **£5–7**.
- **The EU 14-day withdrawal right makes returns a structural cost in the EU and UK.** Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive, the seller can pass the direct cost of returning goods to the consumer only if it informed them beforehand. Returns of China-direct EU parcels do not recover the €3/€2 fees (the handling fee is non-refundable; see section 1).
