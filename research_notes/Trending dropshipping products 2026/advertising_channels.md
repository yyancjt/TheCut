All figures below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from full-text reading. Almost every benchmark here is published by an agency, SaaS vendor or marketplace with a commercial interest; the most defensible aggregated datasets are Triple Whale (40,000+ brands, first-party pixel), Lebesgue (ecommerce-specific, by country), smec (EUR 650M European Google spend) and Klaviyo (183,000 customers). ## 1. Paid-platform benchmarks (CPM, CPC, CTR, CVR, ROAS/CPA) — US vs UK vs EU, plus Q4/Black Friday

### Takeaway
On aggregated first-party data, Google Ads has the best median ROAS (about 3.2x). Meta comes next (about 1.9x, median CPA about $39), then TikTok (about 1.5x). TikTok and Pinterest CPMs are lowest, but their CPMs don't turn into cheaper customers. Meta CPMs are about $15–16 in the US. They are 25–60% lower in Europe: UK about $12, Germany about $9, France, Spain and Italy about $6–7, Poland about $5.5. Since July 2026, though, Meta adds a 2–5% location fee in the UK, FR, IT, ES, AT and TR, and lower EU consent rates degrade tracking. Around Black Friday, Meta CPMs spike by roughly 50–110%, while cost per purchase often falls.

### Inferences
- Break-even ROAS is roughly 1 ÷ gross margin after COGS and shipping. Typical dropshipping margins of 30–40% imply break-even ROAS of about 2.5–3.3x. That is above the Meta (1.88) and TikTok (about 1.5) first-party medians, so the median account loses money on first purchase. Paid social only works for dropshippers with outlier creative, higher AOV (bundles), or post-purchase LTV (email/SMS).
- At a Meta median CPA of about $39, a product under about $40–50 AOV with thin margins is structurally hard to sell through cold Meta traffic. Impulse gadgets need either TikTok/organic virality or bundles that raise AOV.
- Europe is cheaper per impression on Meta (UK about 27% below US, Southern Europe about 60% below, Poland about 65% below per Lebesgue). The net advantage is reduced by the 2–5% location fees in UK/FR/IT/ES/AT, lower consent rates (Q6), and multilingual creative costs. Lower CPM also often comes with lower purchasing power and AOV. Southern and Eastern Europe are cheap test markets for creative, but not necessarily cheaper per customer.
- The TikTok CPM decline (Triple Whale −24%) came with a CVR decline (−37%). Cheap reach did not translate into cheaper customers.
- European Google Shopping CPCs (smec €0.34 median) are about half US levels ($0.66), with higher reported CVR (about 3.5% vs 1.9%). On these numbers, Google Shopping in the EU may be among the cheapest paid acquisition routes for a seller with a clean Merchant Center account. Methodologies differ, so this needs testing.
- For BFCM: costs per purchase often fall despite higher CPMs. That favours sellers who already have warm audiences and email lists; it does not favour new stores testing products. New dropshippers should generally avoid testing new products in the 2 weeks around Black Friday.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2026 Meta/TikTok CPM data surfaced for the Netherlands, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Czechia or Romania. The Lebesgue table only surfaced US/UK/DE/SE/FR/ES/IT/PL in extracts, and the full page could not be fetched.
- No independent, country-split ROAS/CPA data for Europe was found on any platform.
- No first-party Meta, TikTok, Pinterest or Microsoft benchmark publications were found. Advantage+ campaign-type medians from an aggregated, non-vendor source are missing.

- The effect of Meta's EU less-personalized-ads/DMA changes on 2026 CPMs or reach is not quantified anywhere found.
- Black Friday 2026 data (the event is 27 Nov 2026) is not yet available; all BF figures are 2025.

## 2. Organic and creator-led channels: cost and results (organic short-form, UGC, TikTok Shop affiliates, influencers; US vs EU)

### Takeaway
Content-based acquisition is the cheapest entry point. Practitioner consensus is to start with organic short-form, then boost proven posts. US UGC videos cost about $150–200 each (median about $154–175 paid). US micro-influencer feed posts cost about $100–750. In Europe, micro-influencers are quoted at €150–1,200 per post, with the UK highest and Eastern Europe 40–60% cheaper. TikTok Shop affiliate commission averages about 13% in the US and is 10–20% in the UK. Creator affiliates generate 54–87% of top-shop GMV in EU TikTok Shop markets.

### Inferences
- In the EU, TikTok Shop is primarily an affiliate-creator channel: 72–87% of top-shop GMV in DE/FR/ES comes from affiliates. A small seller's cost there is mostly commission (variable), not upfront media. This is attractive for cash-constrained sellers, but requires local (EU-warehoused) stock and compliance (see Q6).
- Paying a creator $150–200 for one UGC video is roughly the cost of 10–13K US Meta impressions. Several videos are needed per product test because only a minority hit, so a realistic creative budget for one product test is about $500–1,000 with human UGC.
- European creator costs are not dramatically lower than US costs in the UK, DACH or France. They are meaningfully lower in Southern and Eastern Europe, where the audience also has lower AOV.

### Gaps

- No reliable data on YouTube/Instagram product-seeding return rates.

## 3. AI-generated creative, AI UGC/avatars and disclosure rules (2026)

### Takeaway
AI avatar and UGC tools cut cost per video by about 90%+ (about $11 per finished video, or roughly $44 per usable cut once rejects are counted, vs about $150–500 for a human creator). Vendor-reported performance is 5–10% lower conversion than human UGC; independent tests are absent. Disclosure rules tightened in 2026:
- TikTok requires an AIGC toggle for fully or significantly AI-generated ads.
- Meta auto-labels ads made with its tools and reportedly detects third-party AI content since June 2026.
- EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2 Aug 2026, mainly for deepfakes and realistic synthetic people.
- New York's synthetic-performer ad-disclosure law took effect June 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Arcads pricing: about $11 per video ($110/month for 10; $220/month for 20) — [AI Funnel Insider](https://aifunnelinsider.com/arcads-ai-review-2026/); [EZUGC](https://www.ezugc.ai/blog/arcads-ai)
- Human UGC: $150–500 per video including edit (Hoox, Mar 2026); Collabstr average about $185 per video in 2026 — [Okane Land](https://okaneland.com/study/ai-ugc-ad-economics/); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-human-ugc-meta-ads-roas-2026) (attribution approximate)
- Hidden costs: "the $2 video" ends up about $44 per usable cut once three rejects per winner are added — [Okane Land](https://okaneland.com/study/ai-ugc-ad-economics/)
- Performance claims (vendor):
  - Videotok's 2026 comparison (via Lumigen): optimized AI UGC converts 5–10% lower than human UGC at about 98% lower cost — [Lumigen](https://lumigen.app/blog/ai-avatar-ugc-ads-2026/)
  - Kantar 2026 (via Playad): creative-effectiveness gap of about 11 percentile points — [Playad](https://playad.ai/blog/comparisons/ai-avatar-ads-vs-ugc-creators)
  - Playad's view: AI avatars win on cost, speed and test volume; real creators win on trust and lower-funnel conversion for considered purchases — [Playad](https://playad.ai/blog/comparisons/ai-avatar-ads-vs-ugc-creators)
- Creative volume matters for algorithmic campaigns: 50+ creatives reportedly beat fewer than 20 by 3–4x ROAS on GMV Max (vendor citing TikTok case studies) — [Z Media](https://www.z.media/insights/gmv-max-content-strategy-tiktok-shop)
- **Disclosure rules:**
  - Meta: applies an "AI info" label to ads made or significantly edited with its own generative tools; automated detection of third-party AI tools reportedly added 1 June 2026; reportedly signed the EU AI Act Code of Practice on AI-content transparency in July 2026 (single source) — [BGBlur](https://www.bgblur.com/blog/ai-ad-disclosure-meta-google-tiktok-ny-law-platform-policies); [AI Vidia](https://ai-vidia.com/insights/ai-ad-disclosure-rules-2026)
  - TikTok: advertisers must switch on "This ad contains AI-generated content" for fully AI-generated or significantly AI-modified ads, including voice cloning — [FireplaceUGC](https://fireplaceugc.com/blog/ai-ad-disclosure-rules); [Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/ai-influencer-disclosure-rules-by-platform)
  - EU AI Act Art. 50 (from 2 Aug 2026): deployers must disclose deepfakes (realistic content resembling real people, places or events); providers must machine-mark outputs. Tools already on the market reportedly have until 2 Dec 2026 to add machine-readable marks (attributed to the 2026 Digital Omnibus). The Act does not ban synthetic presenters — [Billo – EU AI Act](https://billo.app/blog/eu-ai-act/); [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/eu-ai-act-article-50-ad-creative-disclosure-may-2026-deployer-obligations-watermarking-august-2-enforcement); [Oppye](https://www.oppye.com/blog/ai-generated-ads-label-eu-ai-act)
  - New York synthetic-performer disclosure law applies to ads since 9 June 2026 — [BGBlur](https://www.bgblur.com/blog/ai-ad-disclosure-meta-google-tiktok-ny-law-platform-policies)
- UK: the ASA/CAP/CMA 2026 influencer guide says reviews must reflect an influencer's genuine experience, otherwise they are fake reviews under the DMCC Act — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/10/23/new-influencer-guidance-from-the-asa-cap-and-cma-what-has-changed-and-what-to-d-102o6ar). An AI avatar giving a "testimonial" is a direct risk under this logic (inference, see below).

### Inferences
- For small dropshippers, the main economic benefit of AI creative is cheaper testing of hooks and angles at volume, which matters most on algorithm-driven campaigns (Advantage+, GMV Max, PMax/Demand Gen). Winning concepts can then be re-shot with human creators.
- In the UK and EU, AI avatars presented as real customers giving testimonials carry layered risk. They may be fake reviews under the DMCC Act (UK) and EU unfair commercial practices law, and undisclosed deepfakes under AI Act Art. 50. Clear labelling and product demos rather than "I used this" testimonials are the safer use.
- Platform labels (Meta "AI info", TikTok AIGC) may not alone satisfy Art. 50. Sources call this an interpretation, not settled law.

### Gaps
- No independent, controlled (holdout) study of AI-avatar vs human UGC ROAS in 2026; all performance numbers are vendor-reported or second-hand.
- Official Meta and TikTok policy page text could not be fetched; the June 2026 Meta detection date and the July 2026 Code of Practice signature come from secondary blogs.
- Google/YouTube AI-ad disclosure specifics were not confirmed.

## 4. Email/SMS retention benchmarks; marketplaces (Amazon, TikTok Shop, Temu, eBay, Etsy) vs own store

### Takeaway
Automated flows dominate owned-channel revenue. Per Klaviyo's 2026 data, flows are about 5% of email sends but about 41% of email revenue. Abandoned-cart email earns about $6.77 per recipient, and SMS automations earn about 5x more per send than SMS campaigns. In the EU, SMS requires stricter (in Germany effectively double) opt-in. Marketplaces are mostly hostile to classic retail-arbitrage dropshipping: Etsy bans it, while eBay, Amazon and TikTok Shop US forbid shipping from other retailers. They suit private-label or wholesale-sourced sellers rather than AliExpress-style dropshippers.

### Cited Findings
- **Klaviyo 2026 benchmarks** (183,000+ customers, second-hand):
  - Email flows: about 5.3% of email sends but about 41% of email revenue; revenue per recipient (RPR) about 18x campaigns
  - Abandoned cart RPR: email $6.77, SMS $4.48 (top 10%: $13.70 / $8.69)
  - Welcome series RPR: email $5.75, SMS $3.33
  - SMS flows: 7.6% of SMS sends, 45.2% of SMS revenue
  - Sources: [Gosh Digital](https://www.goshdigital.co/blog/klaviyo-flow-revenue-benchmarks); [RevenueFlows](https://revenueflows.ai/blog/klaviyo-benchmarks); [Geysera](https://www.geysera.com/blog/email-marketing/revenue-per-email-the-metric-that-should-have-replaced-open-rates-years-ago)
- Older Klaviyo baseline (2024 report, 2023 data): campaign RPR $0.11 (top 10% $0.95); all flows $1.94. Some blogs give flow RPR as low as $0.25–0.89, a conflict — [AskNeedle](https://www.askneedle.com/blog/klaviyo-email-benchmarks-for-ecommerce-open-rates-ctr-revenue-per-send); [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/email-marketing-benchmarks-ecommerce-2026)
- **SMS:**
  - Omnisend 2025 data (321M texts, 150,000 brands): CTR 12.39% for campaigns, 20.34% for automations; conversion 0.12% for campaigns, about 0.77% for automations — [MessageFlow](https://messageflow.com/blog/sms-marketing-benchmarks/); [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/digital-marketing-statistics/)
  - Klaviyo SMS click rate: 5.6% campaigns, 9.65% flows — [Digital Applied – SMS stats](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/sms-marketing-statistics-2026-open-ctr-data)
  - Postscript: back-in-stock flows reach 36.7–58.7% click rate; automated texts earn $0.74 per send vs $0.15 for one-off campaigns — [Market Intelligence Tools](https://marketintelligencetools.com/reports/sms-marketing-statistics/) (attribution approximate)
  - The median Postscript store converts only about 1.06% of new orders into SMS subscribers — [Market Intelligence Tools](https://marketintelligencetools.com/reports/sms-marketing-statistics/) (attribution approximate)
- **EU SMS compliance:**
  - German courts make double opt-in practically mandatory for SMS; EU texting falls under GDPR — [Omnisend – international SMS](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/international-sms-marketing/)
  - Postscript enforces double opt-in by default and offers EU data residency on request; Omnisend's double opt-in is optional — [Sender](https://www.sender.net/blog/sms-marketing-platforms/)
- **Marketplace policies:**
  - Etsy: "Dropshipping and reselling are not allowed" (craft-supply exceptions). A new seller policy takes effect 9 Jul 2026; a blog claim that it requires DDP for US-bound foreign shipments is unverified — [Etsy seller policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/seller-policy-effective-through-july-8/1489086421092); [AutoDS](https://www.autods.com/blog/etsy-dropshipping/)
  - eBay: dropshipping allowed only from a wholesale supplier or manufacturer, not from another retailer. Fees about 13.25–13.6% plus $0.30–0.40 per order — [SaleHoo](https://www.salehoo.com/learn/ebay-dropshipping)
  - Amazon: you must be seller of record, with your branding on slips and invoices; sourcing from other retailers is prohibited — [EcomCircles](https://ecomcircles.com/blog/amazon-dropshipping-guide-2026/)
  - TikTok Shop US packaging rules (3 Sep 2026) bar dropshipping directly from other retailers. Violations count against Account Health Rating; below 150 points, new listings and campaign access are blocked — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ecommerce-marketing/tiktok-shop-bans-review-bait-inserts-us-packing-rules/)

### Inferences
- For dropshippers paying $30–40+ CPA on Meta, email/SMS flows (welcome, abandoned cart, browse, post-purchase) are the cheapest way to lift blended ROAS. Setting them up before scaling paid spend is high-leverage.
- In the EU, SMS lists will be smaller and slower to build (double opt-in, GDPR), so email carries more of the retention load. Using EU data residency features is advisable.
- Marketplaces such as TikTok Shop and Amazon act as acquisition channels with built-in demand and creator affiliates, but they require inventory-based fulfilment. "Pure" AliExpress-style dropshipping is effectively confined to the seller's own store plus ads and organic.

### Gaps
- No primary-source Klaviyo 2026 benchmark page was read; figures are via secondary blogs and partly conflict.
- No 2026 data on Temu's local-seller program as an acquisition channel for third-party sellers, or on Amazon/Etsy customer-acquisition cost vs own-store CAC.
- No 2026 WhatsApp or Telegram commerce benchmarks were found (see Q5).

## 6. Platform policy and regulatory risks for dropshippers (ad bans, GMC misrepresentation, shipping disclosures, DSA, ASA/CMA, GDPR/Consent Mode)

### Takeaway
The biggest practical risks for a small dropshipper:
- **Google Merchant Center "misrepresentation" suspensions.** Google suspends on detection, without warning. Common triggers are copied supplier content, missing business identity and unrealistic shipping times.
- **Paid-social account restrictions** from poor customer experience.
- **Marketplace retail-arbitrage bans.**
- **UK fake-review/influencer rules**, which the CMA can now enforce with fines of up to 10% of global turnover.
- **EU measurement loss.** Marketing-cookie opt-in rates are about 36–75% depending on country and source. Google's consent modelling only kicks in above about 700 ad clicks per day, so small EU stores see substantially under-reported conversions.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Merchant Center:**
  - Google's misrepresentation policy says violations lead to suspension upon detection, without prior warning. Google may check listings, website, accounts and third-party sources. Reviews take about 7 business days or longer — [Google Merchant Center Help – Misrepresentation](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127?hl=en)
  - Dropshipping is not banned; "low-trust execution" is. The most cited triggers are copied images and descriptions, no supplier authentication, hidden or unrealistic shipping times and missing business identity — [Zenox Media](https://zenoxmedia.com/blog/google-merchant-center-suspension-dropshipping); [GMCSuspension](https://www.gmcsuspension.com/google-merchant-center-dropshipping-suspended.html)
  - A vendor audit claims 96% of suspended accounts lacked complete business identity (self-reported by an appeals vendor). A policy update reportedly came in Oct 2025 — [Trusted Web eServices](https://trustedwebeservices.com/blog/gmc-misrepresentation-policy-update-october-2025/)
  - "Circumventing systems" suspensions follow a separate, harsher path. A claim that Google allows only 3 appeals is unverified — [StubGroup](https://stubgroup.com/blog/fix-your-google-merchant-center-misrepresentation-suspension/); [iComChain](https://www.icomchain.com/merchant-center-misrepresentation-suspension/)
- **Meta/TikTok ad accounts (low-quality evidence):**
  - Blog claims include auto-bans for average delivery over 18 days, mandatory ship-from country disclosure in TikTok dropshipping ads, and a TikTok ban on before/after images from 12 Feb 2026. None were confirmed in official policy — [DoDropshipping](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-marketplace-bans/); [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-dropshipping-ecommerce-ad-compliance-2026); [Dropship-Spy](https://dropship-spy.com/blog/tiktok-ad-account-suspended-avoid-these-fatal-mistakes)
  - Meta reportedly treats stores without clear customer-support information as unsafe — [Elltow](https://www.elltow.pro/post/dropshipping/dropshipping-bans-2026-avoid-recover)
- **EU Digital Services Act:**
  - Art. 28 bars platforms from profiling-based ads to users they know with reasonable certainty are minors. Art. 26 bars targeting by sensitive data. Art. 39 requires very large platforms to keep a public ad repository for at least 1 year after an ad last ran. Duties fall mainly on platforms, not advertisers — [EU DSA Art. 28 text](https://www.eu-digital-services-act.com/Digital_Services_Act_Article_28.html); [Adequate](https://adequate.digital/en/dsa-the-digital-services-act-a-guide-for-marketers/); [Scalevise](https://scalevise.com/resources/eu-digital-services-act-advertising-rules-compliance/)
  - The proposed EU Digital Fairness Act would extend the minors-profiling ban to all traders, with fines of up to 6% of turnover. It is a proposal, not law — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/eus-digital-fairness-act-would-let-brussels-fine-firms-up-to-6-of-turnover/)
- **UK ASA/CAP/CMA:**
  - The influencer guide was updated in 2026 (Oct 2026 edition) and now rests on the DMCC Act 2024 plus the CAP Code. It adds fake-review guidance: a review must reflect the influencer's genuine experience — [ASA influencer guide](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html); [Lewis Silkin, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/10/23/new-influencer-guidance-from-the-asa-cap-and-cma-what-has-changed-and-what-to-d-102o6ar)
  - The CMA can now fine directly: up to 10% of global turnover or £300k, whichever is higher, and up to £300k for individuals (secondary sources). In March 2026 the CMA opened investigations into 5 businesses over review practices, including discounts for 5-star ratings — [LegalEdge](https://www.legaledge.co.uk/2026/07/cma-and-asa-updates-what-scaling-businesses-need-to-know-in-2026/); [Collabios](https://collabios.com/en/blog/asa-cap-code-influencer-compliance-guide-2026)
  - ASA Feb 2026 disclosure research: #gifted and #thanks perform poorly; "Ad" variants and platform labels ("Paid Partnership", "Commission Paid") perform best — [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/10/23/new-influencer-guidance-from-the-asa-cap-and-cma-what-has-changed-and-what-to-d-102o6ar) (attribution approximate)
- **TikTok Shop US:** the 3 Sep 2026 packaging rules ban dropshipping from other retailers and review-bait inserts — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ecommerce-marketing/tiktok-shop-bans-review-bait-inserts-us-packing-rules/)
- **GDPR/ePrivacy consent and tracking:**
  - Searchlab 2026 (drawing on EDPB, Eurobarometer and IAB Europe): EU average marketing-cookie opt-in 46%, down from 54% in 2023; Germany lowest at about 36%, Italy highest at about 63% — [Salman Gul](https://salmangul.com/performance-marketing-opt-in-consent-europe/) (attribution approximate)
  - Didomi 2026: regional average consent 75.1–89.3% (Western Europe lowest); France about 71% — [Mavix Marketing](https://mavixmarketing.com/blog/consent-mode-v2-mandatory-eu-uk-small-business) (attribution approximate)
  - Other German estimates run 40–54% down to under 25% (CookieYes/XICTRON). The sources conflict widely.
  - GA4 reportedly loses 15–40% of conversions in high-reject countries (method unclear) — [Zenku](https://zenku.studio/en/blog/ga4-consent-mode-v2-gdpr/) (attribution approximate)
  - Google's conversion modelling only activates above about 700 ad clicks per day over 7 days per country/domain group, which most small accounts never reach. Google's older (2021) claim is that modelling recovers 70%+ of lost click-to-conversion journeys — [Mavix – skip Consent Mode](https://mavixmarketing.com/blog/google-consent-mode-v2-europe-what-happens-if-you-skip-it); [Meixner](https://meixner-tobias.com/en/blog/server-side-tracking/) (attribution approximate)
  - Vendor posts report that from 15 June 2026 Google Signals no longer backs up ad data collection, so the consent banner alone determines what Google receives. This is unverified against Google docs — [Seresa](https://seresa.io/blog/consent-mode-v2-deadline-june-2026-what-happens-when-ga4-leaves-google-ads)
- **Meta location fees** (Q1) are a cost rather than a compliance risk, but they are EU/UK-specific — [Webtopia](https://www.webtopia.co/blog/meta-location-fees-2026-uk-eu-dtc-brands)

### Inferences
- Before running Google Shopping, a dropshipper needs:
  - Original product photos/video and rewritten descriptions
  - A visible legal business identity: name, address, contact details, and a VAT/company number for EU/UK
  - Accurate shipping-time and returns pages that match the feed
  - A documented supplier relationship

  Without these, GMC suspension is the most likely failure mode, and it also kills the free-listings/AI Mode channel.
- Small EU stores below Google's modelling threshold, and on Meta with low consent, will see platform-reported ROAS understate reality. Server-side tracking (Conversions API, enhanced conversions) and blended MER (total revenue ÷ total ad spend) become essential for EU decision-making. Comparing US vs EU channel ROAS on platform dashboards will be biased against the EU.
- Creator programs in the UK need "Ad" or platform paid-partnership labels and genuine product experience. Paying for 5-star reviews or discounting for reviews is now a direct CMA fining risk.

### Gaps
- Official Meta and TikTok ad-policy text on dropshipping, delivery times and "unacceptable business practices" could not be retrieved. All delivery-time ban claims are unverified.
- No quantitative data on ad-account ban rates for dropshippers.
- Germany's consent rate is highly uncertain (under 25% to 54% across sources). No primary EDPB/Didomi report was read.
- EU (non-UK) influencer disclosure enforcement in 2026 was not researched in depth: Germany's UWG case law, France's 2023 influencer law, and the Netherlands' ACM/Mediawet influencer register were not covered.

## 7. Recommended channel strategies by budget and product type

### Takeaway
Practitioner sources converge on a staged sequence:
1. **$0–500/month:** organic short-form (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) plus free Google listings and email capture, using content to validate products.
2. **About $500–3k/month:** one paid channel at $10–20/day, usually Meta (Advantage+) or TikTok Spark Ads boosting proven organic posts. Add Google Shopping/PMax for search-demand products, plus email/SMS flows.
3. **$3k+/month:** diversify into Google, creators/UGC at volume (including AI-assisted testing), and TikTok Shop affiliates or GMV Max where fulfilment allows. Judge success on blended MER and incremental ROAS, not platform ROAS.

Product type changes the order:
- **Impulse/viral gadgets** fit TikTok and organic first.
- **Considered purchases** fit Google Search/Shopping, YouTube, Pinterest and Reddit, plus email nurture.
- **Consumables** fit Meta plus email/SMS subscriptions, where LTV justifies the first-order CPA.

### Cited Findings
- Practitioner guidance:
  - Organic short-form is the recommended start for tight budgets; start organic TikTok if capital is under about $1,500 — [Funnelish](https://funnelish.com/blog/what-is-organic-dropshipping); [Ryze – TikTok dropshipping](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/tiktok-dropshipping) (attribution approximate)
  - Begin paid on one channel at $5–10/day so a small budget isn't spread thin; test campaigns at $10–20/day on Meta or TikTok — [Adeagle](https://adeagle.app/blog/dropshipping-ads/); [Dropshipping Champions](https://dropshippingchampions.com/blog/how-to-market-a-dropshipping-store) (attribution approximate)
  - Turn winning organic videos into Spark Ads; allocate 10–20% of revenue to ads once a product proves itself — [USAdrop](https://usadrop.com/organic-dropshipping-free-traffic-strategies-that/); [VelocitySpy](https://velocityspy.com/blog/tiktok-organic-vs-paid-ads-dropshipping/)
  - These sources are dropshipping tool vendors with an interest in the answer.
- A claimed TikTok minimum of $50/day per campaign and $20/day per ad group is unverified — [TikAdSuite](https://tikadsuite.com/blog/minimum-budget-for-tiktok-ads/) (attribution approximate)
- Platform ROAS medians to anchor channel choice (Triple Whale): Google 3.18, Pinterest 2.06, Meta 1.88, TikTok 1.47–1.51 — [Triple Whale – good ROAS](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/whats-a-good-roas)
- PMax/GMV Max headline ROAS overstates incremental results: PMax incremental about 2–3.5x vs 3–6x reported; GMV Max incremental 30–50% below headline — [NA Media Experts](https://www.namediaexperts.com/blog-posts/google-performance-max-benchmarks-2026-roas-cpa-conversion-rates); [TopGrowth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/tiktok-gmv-max-ads/)
- GMV Max works best when organic content and affiliates already drive sales (UK test evidence) — [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2026/07/a-2-week-test-on-new-tiktok-gmv-max-ads/)
- Retention leverage: Klaviyo flows earn about 18x campaign RPR; abandoned-cart email about $6.77 RPR — [Gosh Digital](https://www.goshdigital.co/blog/klaviyo-flow-revenue-benchmarks)

### Inferences
These are synthesised from the findings above, not taken from a single source.
- **$0–500/month:**
  - Content: post 1–3 organic short videos daily per product. Spend money on 2–4 cheap UGC or AI-UGC videos ($11–200 each) rather than media.
  - Free channels: enable Merchant Center free listings, which also feed AI Mode, and join Perplexity merchants (US). Set up Klaviyo/Omnisend welcome and abandoned-cart flows on free tiers.
  - Market choice: for the EU/UK, consider TikTok Shop with local stock and affiliates on commission (10–20%) instead of media spend.
- **$500–3k/month:**
  - One paid channel. Meta Advantage+ is the default for US and UK (median CTR 2.39%, broad reach). TikTok Spark Ads suit impulse products with a proven organic hook.
  - Google Shopping/PMax for products with existing search demand. This is especially attractive in the EU, where median Shopping CPC is about €0.34.
  - Use cheap EU markets (ES/IT/PL CPM about $5.5–6.7) for creative testing. Watch the location fees, VAT/IOSS costs and lower AOV.
- **$3k+/month:**
  - Add a second or third channel (Google if you started on Meta, or vice versa) and creative volume (20–50+ variants/month, AI-assisted).
  - Run TikTok Shop affiliates plus GMV Max if fulfilment qualifies.
  - Test Reddit DPA (Shopify integration) or Pinterest for considered or visual categories. Invest in server-side tracking for the EU.
- **Product-type fit:**
  - Impulse/viral gadget (low AOV): organic TikTok/Reels, Spark Ads, TikTok Shop affiliates. Meta only with bundles that lift AOV above about $50–60, because Meta's median CPA is about $39.
  - Considered purchase (higher AOV, research-led): Google Search/Shopping/PMax, YouTube/Demand Gen, Pinterest, Reddit communities, AI-search/GEO feed hygiene, longer email nurture.
  - Consumable/replenishable: Meta plus email/SMS (replenishment and subscription flows), where repeat purchases make a first-order CPA above break-even acceptable.
- **US vs EU/UK sequencing:**
  - US: deepest TikTok Shop market ($11.8B H1 2026), highest CPMs, and access to AI-search shopping surfaces.
  - EU/UK: Meta CPMs 25–65% lower; much cheaper Google Shopping clicks; TikTok Shop now in 10 markets, heavily affiliate-driven.
  - EU/UK frictions: 2–5% Meta location fees, consent-driven measurement loss, multilingual creative, and stricter SMS opt-in.

### Gaps
- No credible, independent (non-vendor) dataset compares channel CAC by monthly budget tier for small stores. The budget tiers above are a synthesis, not a measured benchmark.
- No data on profitability rates of new dropshipping stores by channel.
- No product-type-specific CAC benchmarks by country for the EU.
