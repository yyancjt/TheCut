# T7 — Software substrate & protocol shifts 2024–2026, and what they enable downstream in Western Europe

*Search-backed version, 2026-09-06. Replaces `T7-software-substrate-draft.md` (which was written with zero web searches and GitHub-only evidence). Audience: a 1–3 person technical founder, <€100k, 6–9 months to v1, Western Europe, who wants second-order opportunities for end users, SMEs, physical businesses and the public sector — not B2B SaaS, copilots, or "AI agent for X".*

## 0. How to read this

- **Evidence tags:** `[S]` = confirmed by a cited search result this session; `[K]` = prior knowledge, unverified; `[I]` = inference.
- **Method:** ~167 web searches on 2026-09-06. WebFetch is blocked for most domains by the egress policy (one GitHub fetch succeeded), so this rests on search-result snippets — treat single-source numbers as directional, not audited.
- **Cross-track:** energy/HEMS → T3; sensors and device data → T4; EUDI wallet, age verification, payments → T6; agent-legibility from the AI-capability side → T1 (F5); regulatory dates → T2.
- **The one-paragraph read.** Between September 2025 and September 2026 the agentic-commerce stack consolidated and then *retreated from checkout*: the winning pattern is "discover in AI, buy on the merchant's site." Meanwhile Google quietly shipped the thing that matters to European physical businesses — agentic *booking* — wired to a partner layer (Reserve with Google) most single-location European SMEs cannot reach. Separately, on-device AI became a shipped browser/OS platform, local-first sync engines hit 1.0, and European digital sovereignty became procurement law with dates (SEAL, CADA, SecNumCloud 3.2, ODF/PDF-UA mandatory in Germany, 2.5M French workstations moving to Linux). The best solo-founder opportunities sit where a *mandate with a date* meets a *supplier shortage*, not where a protocol is exciting.
## F1. Agentic commerce consolidated on protocols — and abandoned in-chat checkout

**(a) What changed.** OpenAI + Stripe published the Agentic Commerce Protocol on 2025-09-29 and launched Instant Checkout in ChatGPT (US, Etsy then Shopify) [S](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout). Google announced AP2 (2025-09-16, 60+ partners incl. Nexi and Revolut) [K/S], then the **Universal Commerce Protocol** with Shopify at NRF on **2026-01-11**, joined by Etsy, Wayfair, Target, Walmart and later Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce and Stripe [S](https://www.charle.co.uk/articles/shopify-ucp/). Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol landed 2025-10-14, Mastercard Agent Pay 2025-04-29 [S](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15192003-mastercard-agent-pay-vs-visa-trusted-agent-2026-compared). **Then OpenAI reversed:** in March 2026 it scaled back Instant Checkout and moved purchasing into Apps and back to merchant sites, after Walmart measured in-chat checkout converting at roughly a third of on-site [S](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/openai-revamps-shopping-experience-in-chatgpt-after-instant-checkout.html) [S](https://www.hypotenuse.ai/blog/chatgpts-instant-checkout-the-next-phase-of-agentic-commerce). European plumbing exists — Mollie is ACP-ready, Adyen shipped "Adyen Agentic" on 2026-06-16 supporting UCP/ACP/AP2 but **US-enterprise-limited at launch** [S](https://www.adyen.com/press-and-media/adyen-agentic) [S](https://fintech.global/2025/11/24/mollie-prepares-european-businesses-for-agentic-commerce/) — and Visa took agentic payments live with **30+ European issuers** in Paris on 2026-07-02 [S](https://theindustryspread.com/visa-agentic-payments-live-30-european-issuers/).

**(b) Non-obvious implication.** The protocol war was over the *checkout*, the least valuable part. What survived is the **feed** (machine-readable inventory/price/policy) and the **identity** (is this agent allowed, who pays if it is wrong). A European SME does not need ACP/UCP checkout; it needs to be legible and transactable enough that an agent routes a human to it. [I]

**(c–e) Who pays / gap / feasibility.** In the UK only 15% of top-100 retailers say their payment systems are agent-ready while 49% invest in agentic AI; consumer demand in DE/FR/UK is real (84% use AI tools, 63% to compare brands and prices) [S](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/europes-agentic-commerce-moment-decision-influence-is-here-execution-is-coming). But platform vendors absorb this for *product* commerce (F2). Do not build ACP plumbing. **Avoid.**
## F2. The real shift for physical Europe: agentic *booking* shipped — and the long tail gets phoned

**(a) What changed.** UCP's maintainers concede there is **"no native concept of a time-slot or availability window in the current fulfillment spec"**, while the roadmap lists "new verticals beyond Shopping (e.g. Travel, Services)" [S, fetched](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/discussions/93). Google did not wait: agentic **restaurant booking rolled out worldwide via AI Mode on 2026-04-10** (8 new markets incl. UK, Canada, Australia) [S](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-mode-agentic-restaurant-booking/) [S](https://ppc.land/googles-ai-mode-now-books-uk-restaurants-meet-the-8-partner-platforms/); at I/O 2026 it extended agentic booking to **home repair, beauty and pet care** (US first, summer 2026) [S](https://www.capconvert.com/learn/blog/google-agentic-booking-local-services); UCP itself reached **hotels and food delivery on 2026-05-20** with Booking.com, Expedia, DoorDash and Uber Eats [S](https://www.paz.ai/blog/ucp-universal-cart-hotels-food-delivery-vertical-expansion); Google Maps added agentic ordering and hotel booking in August 2026 [S](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/google-maps-adds-agentic-features-including-food-ordering-and-hotel-bookings/). Local Services Ads booking partners went from ~20 to **500+** [S](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-lsa-expands-booking-partners-41945.html).

**The chokepoint:** Reserve with Google works only through a **supported B2B scheduling provider** serving multiple businesses, with real-time availability, **sub-second** query response, online cancellation and Actions Center integration [S](https://developers.google.com/actions-center/verticals/reservations/bl/overview) [S](https://support.google.com/reserve/answer/9172607). For restaurants without one, businesses with automated booking capture AI-initiated bookings seamlessly, while the rest "need a human to respond every time — **and if no one answers, Google moves to the next available business**" [S](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2026/04/google-introduces-ai-powered-restaurant-booking-as-search-evolves-into-action/).

**(b) Non-obvious implication.** This is not "SMEs need an AI receptionist". It is **structural re-intermediation**: whoever is the *scheduling provider of record* for a vertical in a country becomes the agent-facing supply side; everyone else falls to the end of a queue. Hospitality already shows the pattern — Booking.com and Expedia dominate AI travel recommendations because they expose standardised machine-readable content, while independents "only exist where platforms speak for them" [S](https://tryxlr8.ai/news/ai-agents-travel-booking-data-infrastructure-2026). Expedia is shipping a B2B **MCP server** [S](https://nomadlawyer.org/expedia-agentic-ai-b2b-server-may-2026); hotel CRS Simple Booking shipped MCP connectors GA in June 2026 [S](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4132950/). Nothing equivalent exists for European physios, driving schools, workshops, Sachverständige, vets or small hotels.

**(c–d) Pain, payers, exact gap.** Vertical booking vendors in DE/FR/NL/ES/IT lose their customers' demand channel if they are not an Actions Center partner and not UCP/MCP-legible. Anchors: ~76,845 full-service restaurants in Germany [S](https://www.ibisworld.com/germany/industry/full-service-restaurants/911/); Dutch hospitality is 98% SMEs [S](https://wifitalents.com/netherlands-hospitality-industry-statistics/); German hair salons alone were €9.88bn output in 2023, France €5.62bn [S](https://hub.claight.com/statistics/hair-salons-in-eu) — a country×vertical wedge is tens of thousands of businesses. Doctolib (500k+ professionals, 120k in Germany, "agentic" workflows by 2027) [S](https://www.healthcare.digital/single-post/doctolib-s-potential-3-year-strategic-outlook-2026-to-2028-ipo-ai-and-international-expansion), Treatwell and Quandoo will serve their own bases; generic MCP calendar servers are single-tenant toys [S](https://chatforest.com/guides/best-calendar-scheduling-mcp-servers/). **Gap: a multi-tenant, sub-second availability/booking backend for one underserved European vertical that satisfies Actions Center requirements, exposes MCP, is UCP-shaped once time-slots land, and carries deposit/no-show policy plus agent verification.**

**(e) Feasibility.** 4–6 months for one vertical if you buy calendars via adapters into the practice/POS software SMEs already run. Moat: the Actions Center partnership itself (Google vets providers), adapter coverage, association endorsement. Risk: Google extends a "connectivity model" letting independents join without a provider [S](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2026/04/google-introduces-ai-powered-restaurant-booking-as-search-evolves-into-action/) — so pick a vertical Google is not in; regulated/medical categories are explicitly **excluded** from Reserve with Google [S](https://gainlocal.net/blog/local-seo/what-businesses-are-eligible-for-reserve-with-google/).
## F3. Local businesses are becoming invisible to assistants, and the measurement is now public

**(a) What changed.** AI Mode reached the EU in 2026 — Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Italy, Spain and Poland first, **France on 2026-07-22** [S](https://aioseo.fr/en/ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-landed-in-france-on-july-22-2026-two-months-before-thepromised-deadline/) [S](https://www.eology.net/news/google-ki-modus). AI Overviews appeared on ~48% of searches by March 2026 (+58% YoY) with a measured 58% CTR drop on the top organic result [S](https://www.seo-kreativ.de/en/blog/google-ai-overviews-updates-2026-en/). Uberall (May 2026): **83% of restaurant locations are entirely invisible in AI-generated recommendations**; SOCi: AI recommends ~1.2% of local business locations [S](https://openlens.com/blog/en/ai-visibility-restaurants-2026). team.blue's *State of European Business 2026* (10,000+ businesses, 32 countries): only **5.5%** feel completely in control of how customers discover them, and small businesses are twice as likely to feel unprepared [S](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260616606679/en/). Only ~23% of German SMEs use structured data at all [S](https://www.internetagentur-hildesheim.de/en/blog/ai-overviews-local-businesses-visible-2026/).

**(b–e) Implication, payers, gap.** The buyer does not want "GEO", it wants bookings; visibility work only pays when it terminates in a machine-completable action (F2) [I]. Uberall (1.4M locations, 1,800 customers, UB-I agentic product, GEO Studio), SOCi, Yext, Reputation and Birdeye all target multi-location brands — **Uberall's entry package requires a minimum of 25 locations** and pricing is sales-call-gated [S](https://ampifire.com/blog/uberall-pricing-reviews-alternatives-is-this-multi-location-market-platform-worth-it/) [S](https://uberall.com/en-us/resources/blog/what-is-ub-i). The gap is the **1–3 location, non-English business**: self-serve, €19–49/month, distributed through the POS, booking and web agencies they already pay. Easy to build, hard to distribute, and only defensible bundled with F2's transactional endpoint — alone it is a marketing-tools business and will be commoditised (F5).
## F4. Agent identity shipped before the standard did — and bots crossed 50% of the web

**(a) What changed.** Cloudflare Radar puts automated requests at **57.5% of HTML traffic** vs 42.5% human, with the crossover called on 2026-06-03 [S](https://optimizewithsanwal.com/ai-agent-traffic-cloudflare-bots-surpass-humans/); HUMAN Security measured **agentic** traffic growing 7,851% in a year [S](https://theaicareerlab.com/blog/ai-agents-majority-web-traffic-2026). Web Bot Auth (RFC 9421 HTTP Message Signatures, Ed25519 keys at `/.well-known/http-message-signatures-directory`) is **verified in production by Cloudflare, AWS WAF, Akamai, HUMAN and Vercel even though its IETF working group had adopted no documents as of 2026-08-12** [S](https://nerdleveltech.com/web-bot-auth-ietf-standard-agent-verification), and Cloudflare wired it into Visa/Mastercard/Amex agent authentication [S](https://www.coronium.io/blog/web-bot-auth-verifiable-ai-agents-2026). Legally, the US 9th Circuit **overturned** Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's Comet agent [S](https://www.engadget.com/2230471/perplexity-has-successfully-overturned-amazon-injunction-on-its-ai-shopping-bot/); in the EU the lever is different — the CJEU has held site operators can enforce anti-scraping terms via ordinary contract law even absent database-right protection [S](https://cloro.dev/blog/website-scraping-legal/).

**(b) Implication.** Two opposite problems land on the same small business: **agents it wants (buyers) get blocked** — Cloudflare flips new and free zones to blocking mixed-use AI crawlers by default from **2026-09-15** [S](https://chudi.dev/blog/cloudflare-block-ai-crawlers-september-15) — while **agents it doesn't want get through**: agent-driven synthetic account creation and refund abuse now top merchant fraud rankings, with 64% reporting rising first-party misuse [S](https://www.trustsphere.ai/post/when-shopping-agents-open-accounts-autonomous-ai-is-industrialising-synthetic-and-duplicate-sign-up).

**(c–e) Pain, gap, feasibility.** Anyone taking bookings or deposits online, especially where a no-show costs a seat. In Germany an AI *calling* a business is separately constrained: recording without all-party consent is criminal, and from **2026-08-02** AI Act Art. 50 requires unambiguous AI disclosure [S](https://echocall.de/en/blog/eu-ai-act-2026-ki-kennzeichnungspflicht) [S](https://ainora.lt/blog/ai-voice-agent-dsgvo-compliance-germany-austria). CDN-level verification is becoming a free checkbox; the *policy* layer — accept verified agents, require deposits from unverified ones, log the mandate, disclose AI, keep the audit trail — is not, and belongs inside F2's product rather than a standalone business.
## F5. llms.txt is a dud; "agent readiness" got a free scoreboard

**(a) What changed.** A 300,000-domain study puts llms.txt adoption at **10.13%**; across 500M+ AI bot visits in a 90-day window only **408** requests targeted `/llms.txt`, and one analysis found 97% of published files received **no** requests in a month. Google's Gary Illyes said on the record (July 2025) that Google does not support it, and as of Q1 2026 no major lab had committed to reading it [S](https://aiseousa.com/blog/what-is-llms-txt-does-it-work) [S](https://geojacker.com/llms-txt). Meanwhile Cloudflare launched a free public **Agent Readiness Score** on **2026-04-17** (isitagentready.com), checking robots.txt/sitemap/Link headers, Markdown content negotiation, Content Signals, Web Bot Auth, Agent Skills index, API catalog and MCP Server Card. Result: top-100 sites average 55%; **only 3.9% support Markdown content negotiation, 4% declare AI usage preferences, and fewer than 15 sites globally publish an MCP Server Card** [S](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agent-readiness/) [S](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/all-you-need-to-know-about-cloudflares-agent-readiness-score/574226/).

**(b–e) Implication.** The audit is now free and vendor-supplied, so agent-readiness audits as an agency service have a ~12-month shelf life. The *scarce* item is the last checklist row: an MCP Server Card or API catalog that does something transactional — fewer than 15 sites on earth have one. Use the free score as a lead-generation wedge into F2/F3. **Do not build a GEO agency.** [I]

## F6. MCP became infrastructure, and a European state shipped an official public-data endpoint

**(a) What changed.** MCP SDK downloads hit **97M/month by March 2026** (≈970× in 18 months); Stacklok's 2026 survey found **41%** of software organisations with MCP servers in limited or broad production and 28% of the Fortune 500 deployed; the official registry listed **9,652** servers in May 2026 [S](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics-2026-model-context-protocol) [S](https://mcpmanager.ai/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics/). A2A passed **150 organisations**, shipped 1.0 with signed Agent Cards, and is in Azure AI Foundry, Bedrock AgentCore and Vertex AI [S](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year). Critically: **France's Etalab launched the official data.gouv.fr MCP server on 2026-02-25**, exposing **74,000+ datasets** through seven tools, MIT-licensed, hosted at `mcp.data.gouv.fr/mcp` with **no API key** [S](https://rits.shanghai.nyu.edu/ai/france-opens-74000-public-datasets-to-ai-agents-via-official-mcp-server). The Commission is funding the pattern (Digital Europe call DIGITAL-2026-BESTUSE-MCP-09-INNOV-ADMIN, Jan–May 2026) [S](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/digital/wp-call/2026/call-fiche_digital-2026-bestuse-mcp-09_en.pdf).

**(b–e) Implication, payers, gap.** The consumer of a public-data MCP endpoint is not a developer but a notary, architect, estate agent, tax adviser or municipal clerk asking an assistant a question and expecting a *sourced* answer — which reframes open data from a portal into a per-seat professional subscription [I]. Security is the systemic weakness: only **8.5%** of MCP servers implement the mandatory OAuth 2.1 requirement, and the spec defers enterprise security to extensions [S](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics-2026-model-context-protocol) [S](https://goteleport.com/blog/complicating-mcp-enterprise/). Nothing hosted, cited and SLA'd exists for German, Dutch or Italian registers; community servers cover UK Land Registry and French IGN cadastral parcels [S](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/pipeworx-io/mcp-landregistry-uk). See F7 and Top-6 #4.
## F7. The High-Value-Dataset mandate is real, unevenly implemented, and Germany's company register is the sore thumb

**(a) What changed.** Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/138 has bound all Member States since June 2024 across six categories — geospatial, earth observation/environment, meteorological, statistics, **companies and company ownership**, mobility — requiring free machine-readable access **via APIs and bulk download**, company data under CC BY 4.0; implementation "lacks evenness" [S](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/high-value-datasets-questions-and-answers) [S](https://data.europa.eu/en/news-events/news/high-value-datasets-what-has-changed-and-what-will-come-next). Concretely: **handelsregister.de has no official public REST API, a hard limit of 60 queries/hour, no bulk download and no availability SLA** [S](https://zephira.ai/germany-handelsregister-the-complete-guide-to-german-company-registry-data-in-2026/). German **geodata** did open: since 2024-06-09 all 16 Länder publish ALKIS/terrain/orthophotos as open data, though "the situation remains very heterogeneous", and Bavaria brings a revised fee regulation on **2026-07-01** [S](https://www.ciss.de/open-data/) [S](https://learn.opengeoedu.de/opendata/vorlesung/offene-geodaten/amtliche-geodaten). The Netherlands is cleanest (PDOK/BAG free, ~100% uptime, old BAG OGC API retired **2026-07-15**) [S](https://www.pdok.nl/-/nieuwe-bag-ogc-api-features); France added 20,000+ datasets with quality indicators and standardised schemas [S](https://data.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025_odm_factsheet_france.pdf); data.europa.eu now lists **1.7M+ datasets from 36 countries** [S](https://data.europa.eu/euodp/en/home).

**(b–e) Implication.** The value is not the data but **normalisation, entity resolution and uptime** across 16 Länder × 6 categories × 5 languages, plus a citation trail a professional can put in a file. Germany's non-delivery on company registers is the most monetisable gap precisely because incumbents (North Data, Creditreform, handelsregister.ai, OpenRegister) already prove willingness to pay for that layer [S](https://openregister.de/en/api) [S](https://handelsregister.ai/en). See Top-6 #4.
## F8. On-device AI in the browser and OS is now a shipped platform, not a demo

**(a) What changed.** **WebGPU reached Baseline in January 2026** — Chrome, Edge, Firefox (Windows + macOS Tahoe) and Safari 26+ all ship it stable, covering macOS Tahoe 26, iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and visionOS 26 [S](https://www.webgpu.com/news/webgpu-hits-critical-mass-all-major-browsers/). **Chrome 148 shipped on 2026-05-05 with the Prompt API stable**, exposing Gemini Nano to *websites* with text, image and audio input and regex/JSON-schema constrained output, alongside Summarizer, Translator, Language Detector, Writer and Rewriter; Trip.com generates flight summaries fully client-side [S](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260506-google-chrome-148/) [S](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/3145/gemini-nano-chrome-built-in-ai-client-side-en). **WebNN is not production-ready** — Chrome 147–149 origin trial only, realistic default 2027 [S](https://www.ddevtools.com/updates/2026-01-webgpu-webnn-browser-ai). Apple's Foundation Models framework "crossed from experimental API to mainstream feature layer" with iOS 26, gained vision at WWDC26 and **was opened to any LLM provider** [S](https://appsops.store/news/apple-foundation-models-ios26-on-device-ai-features) [S](https://dev.to/arshtechpro/wwdc-2026-apple-just-opened-the-foundation-models-framework-to-any-llm-provider-5ejn). Android's ML Kit GenAI APIs on AICore now cover Prompt, Summarization, Proofreading, Rewriting, Image Description and **Speech Recognition**, with Nano 4 in preview [S](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/05/android-ai-intelligence-system.html). Quality caught up: **NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3** beats Whisper large-v3 on the Open ASR Leaderboard at a quarter the size and runs fast on plain CPU across 25 European languages; German WER ~8.3%, French ~9.0% on FLEURS [S](https://openvoxai.com/blog/best-local-stt-transcription-models-2026) [S](https://www.onresonant.com/resources/local-stt-models-2026).

**(b) Implication.** "No bytes leave the device" moved from an architecture argument to a **cost structure**: on-device inference is free at the per-query level once the OS ships the model, so a €9–19/user/month tool can carry ~90% gross margin where cloud-ASR competitors must charge €30–60 [S on cost](https://appsops.store/news/apple-foundation-models-ios26-on-device-ai-features); [I on pricing]. With GDPR and §203 constraints, this inverts the usual "cloud is cheaper" assumption for regulated small buyers. See F9.
## F9. The device-only wedge is *not* doctors — it is §203 professions and public-sector-adjacent work

**(a) What changed.** The clinician ambient-scribe market closed fast: Doctolib and Docplanner/Jameda (Noa) shipped through 2025; Heidi focused on DACH; Tandem, Heidi and **Voize** raised $40–60M rounds in 2026 — Voize took **€50M (Balderton) and reports 200,000+ users across 2,000+ facilities as of May 2026**, with recognition running **on-device** on the phone [S](https://healthtechofftherecord.substack.com/p/3-questions-for-the-european-ai-scribe) [S](https://www.altenheim.net/voize-erhaelt-50-millionen-us-dollar-fuer-ki-gestuetzte-pflegedokumentation/). T5 independently marks clinician scribes as closed.

The **§203 StGB professions** are not served. German law requires any external IT/AI provider handling professional secrets to be bound as a "mitwirkende Person" under §203(3) — a GDPR DPA is explicitly **not** sufficient — plus EU operation and no-training guarantees [S](https://www.agentifizierung.de/softwarekosten-senken/ki-berufsgeheimnistraeger-mandantendaten) [S](https://sehlmeyer-digital.de/blog-ki-steuerkanzlei-203-stgb.html). A June-2026 review of publicly documented §203 undertakings found **five** qualifying tools (Open Telekom Cloud, Logicc, ASCADI, Taxy.io, Beck-Noxtua) [S](https://www.ai-finden.de/ratgeber/ki-tools-par203-stgb-verschwiegenheitsvereinbarung/). The alternative is a self-hosted local LLM: for a 20–50-lawyer firm, **low-to-mid five figures to implement plus low four figures monthly** [S](https://legaldata.tech/lokales-llm-in-kanzlei-einsetzen-vorteile-und-praxistipps/). The public/social sector has the same problem and is only now piloting (Prosoz + Sweden's Intric, sovereign AI transcription for sensitive citizen data in German municipalities) [S](https://www.kommune21.de/k21-meldungen/ki-fuer-sensible-buergerdaten/).

**(b) Implication.** If the model runs on the user's own device there is **no processor and no §203 disclosure to authorise** — the compliance argument collapses to "nothing left the machine", far easier to sell to a Datenschutzbeauftragte than any DPA. That is a legal-architecture advantage, not an AI one. [I]

**(c–d) Pain, payers, gap.** ~100k Steuerberater and small law firms, notaries, Wirtschaftsprüfer, Betreuer, private-practice therapists, school social workers and Jugendamt case workers, plus equivalents in AT/CH/FR/IT — small recurring budgets, and the buyer is a partner, not a CIO. Today the options are enterprise cloud tools with §203 paperwork or DIY local LLMs at €50k+. **Nobody ships a €10–20/user/month, install-and-go, device-only dictation + template product with the §203/DSGVO pack (TOM description, DPIA template, Verschwiegenheitserklärung) in the box.**

**(e) Feasibility.** 3–5 months: PWA or native app on WebGPU + Parakeet/Whisper or Apple Foundation Models, encrypted local storage, vertical templates (Aktennotiz, Mandantengespräch, Hilfeplan, Beratungsprotokoll), WASM fallback. Moat: templates, professional-body endorsement and the compliance pack — not the model. Distribution: Kammern, Steuerberaterverbände, DSB consultants, Fachverlage. Risk: a good-enough free OS dictation feature — mitigate by owning the *document*, not the transcription.
## F10. Local-first crossed into production, and got a sovereignty argument

**(a) What changed.** ElectricSQL hit 1.0 (March 2025); **Zero (Rocicorp) shipped 1.0 in June 2026** after two years of alpha [S](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/06/zero-version-1/); Automerge 3.0 cut memory ~10× with a Rust core, 40–60% smaller documents and sub-millisecond merges; PowerSync ships native SDKs for React Native, Flutter, web and Kotlin Multiplatform over Postgres, MongoDB, Azure DocumentDB, MySQL and SQL Server [S](https://appscale.blog/en/blog/local-first-architecture-crdts-sync-engines-offline-first-2026) [S](https://www.alexcloudstar.com/blog/local-first-software-developer-guide-2026/). At **QCon London 2026 Martin Kleppmann tied local-first directly to European sovereignty**, noting AWS/Azure/GCP hold ~70% of the European market and that EU regions do not solve extraterritoriality [S](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/03/qcon-local-first-sovereignty). ## Top 6 non-obvious opportunities from this track

**1. Be the *scheduling provider of record* for one underserved European vertical.** Google now books restaurants agentically in 200+ countries and is extending to home repair, beauty and pet care — but only through certified B2B scheduling providers meeting Actions Center requirements (multi-business, real-time availability, sub-second response, online cancellation), while UCP still has **no time-slot primitive at all**. That creates a two-tier world: integrated businesses receive structured bookings, the rest get phoned by an AI and, if nobody answers, dropped for the next business. Build the multi-tenant availability backend for a vertical Google's partner list doesn't cover and its policy excludes (regulated/medical is out of Reserve with Google) — Heilmittelerbringer, Sachverständige, driving schools, Kfz-Werkstätten, veterinary, small hotels — with adapters into the practice software those SMEs already run, an MCP endpoint, deposit/no-show policy, and agent verification via Web Bot Auth. Sell white-label to incumbent vertical software and through trade associations. Infrastructure, not an "AI agent for X".

**2. Device-only documentation for §203 StGB professions.** WebGPU is Baseline, Chrome 148 ships Gemini Nano to websites, Apple Foundation Models is mainstream, and Parakeet-class ASR beats Whisper-large at a quarter the size with ~8% German WER — so a €9–19/user/month tool with no server-side inference is a ~90%-margin product. The moat is legal: if nothing leaves the device there is no processor to bind under §203(3), where a GDPR DPA is explicitly insufficient. Clinicians are closed (Doctolib, Jameda Noa, Heidi, Voize at €50M); Steuerberater, small law firms, notaries, Betreuer, therapists, school social workers and Jugendamt case workers are not — their only options are five niche cloud tools or a €50k self-hosted LLM project. Ship vertical templates plus the compliance pack and sell through Kammern and Datenschutzbeauftragte.

**3. The German public-sector document-mandate toolkit (ODF + PDF/UA) — sold to authorities *and their suppliers*.** On 2026-03-18 the IT-Planungsrat made ODF and PDF/UA the only permitted formats across federal, state and municipal administration, transitioning to 2027/28 — while only 9.5% of public-sector PDFs are actually accessible, Schleswig-Holstein's stubborn last 20% is Windows-locked Fachverfahren, DINUM has ordered 2.5M French workstations onto Linux, and German municipalities have 100,000+ unfilled posts and 2–3 applicants per IT vacancy. Build the instrument, not the consultancy: batch conformance checking, auto-remediation of tagging, a VBA/Windows-only-application inventory scanner, ODF conversion QA, and a signed "procurement-legal" report a supplier attaches to a bid. Every private supplier to a German authority becomes a customer — a far larger market than the authorities. Bundle with EAA remediation for private sites.

**4. Hosted, cited, SLA'd agent-ready access to German registers and geodata.** France proved the demand curve with an official state MCP server exposing 74,000 datasets and no API key. Germany did the opposite: handelsregister.de still has no public REST API, a 60-queries-per-hour cap, no bulk download and no SLA, despite company registers being a mandatory High-Value Dataset since June 2024 — while all 16 Länder did open ALKIS/terrain/orthophotos, heterogeneously. The product is normalisation plus provenance: entity resolution across HRB/LEI/USt-IdNr, a cadastral and building layer stitched across Länder, versioned snapshots, every answer citing its source document — as both REST and MCP, priced per professional seat (notaries, tax advisers, architects, estate agents, insolvency administrators), with a white-label "ask our open data" tier for municipalities. The moat is data cleaning and uptime, which compounds; North Data and Creditreform have not shipped agent endpoints.

**5. Centimetre stake-out for open-field trades on free Galileo HAS corrections.** HAS broadcasts free PPP on E6-B at ~20 cm horizontal, with Full Service targeted for Q4 2026 and shipping support from u-blox, Septentrio and Trimble that removes the need for 4G or local RTK infrastructure; German, Dutch and French cadastral geodata is free; Overture supplies the base map as GERS heads to OGC standardisation. That collapses a €10–20k RTK kit plus correction subscription, or a €500/day surveyor call-out, into a €400–1,500 receiver and an app — for landscapers, fencers, drainage and irrigation contractors, vineyards and municipal Bauhöfe. The product is not the receiver: it is trade-specific stake-out UX, per-country cadastre overlays and as-built documentation that survives a dispute. Start where convergence time and canopy don't bite.

**6. EU-only iPhone capabilities as a hardware-adjacent wedge.** The DMA created capabilities a European developer can ship and an American one cannot: HCE NFC in your own app as the in-store default, AirPods-grade proximity pairing for third-party accessories (iOS 26.3, EU only), notification forwarding, Wi-Fi Aware peer-to-peer to cheap ESP32 hardware, and AirDrop/AirPlay alternatives mandated by end-2026 — with unified terms and a flat 5% Core Technology Commission from 2026-10-01. Point that at building access: HOA and Hausverwaltung keys, coworking, gyms, works-council badges, event entry — markets locked today to proprietary fobs. The entitlement and certification friction is the moat. Watch the web-distribution eligibility rules (D&B score, public company, or VC funding), which exclude most bootstrappers.

## Corrections to the draft (`T7-software-substrate-draft.md`)

1. **A reversal was missed.** OpenAI *retired* Instant Checkout in March 2026 and moved purchase flows to Apps / merchant sites after in-chat conversion measured ~⅓ of on-site. Consensus is now "discover in AI, buy on the merchant's site." [S]
2. **"Time-slot commerce got nothing" was right in January and wrong by September.** UCP still has no time-slot primitive, but Google shipped agentic restaurant booking worldwide (2026-04-10), extended UCP to hotels and food delivery (2026-05-20) and announced local-services agentic booking at I/O 2026. The opportunity survives only as *being a certified scheduling provider for verticals Google's partners don't cover* — not a generic "agent front desk". [S]
3. **Draft #4 ("SMEs called by robots") overweighted GitHub stars.** All AI browsers combined are forecast at 1–3% of the 2026 browser market; Comet reached ~10M users; **ChatGPT Atlas stopped being a browser on 2026-08-09**. Bot traffic is 57.5% of HTML requests but overwhelmingly crawlers, not shoppers. [S]
4. **Cloudflare dates were conflated.** Pay Per Crawl launched **2025-07-01**; Content Signals Policy **2025-09-24**; default blocking of mixed-use AI crawlers and the **"Pay Per Use"** rebrand are **2026** (announced 1 July, effective 15 September). [S]
5. **llms.txt was over-credited.** Production crawlers do not read it; Google declined to support it. The draft's "Lighthouse audits sites for one" could not be corroborated — treat as **likely wrong**. The real scoreboard is Cloudflare's Agent Readiness Score (2026-04-17). [S/I]
6. **Apple EU eligibility was overstated.** Web distribution from 2026-10-01 requires **at least one of** a moderate D&B score, public-company status/ownership, or venture funding — a real barrier for bootstrappers. The 5% CTC and removal of Initial Acquisition/Store Services fees are confirmed. [S]
7. **Matter energy was over-credited.** Matter 1.5 adds an electrical-energy-tariff device type, but **no shipping Matter-certified solar-inverter or heat-pump products were confirmed as of July 2026**. Matter energy is a spec, not a market; the binding constraint is a 22% technician vacancy rate. [S]
8. **Local-first: "offline" is not the differentiator.** Offline sync is table stakes in field-service software and German ambulante-Pflege incumbents already do it. The new sellable property is offline *plus* customer-controlled EU data. Also, Zero reached **1.0 in June 2026** (the draft said "not GA"). [S]
9. **EUDI-wallet verifier-as-a-service (draft #7) is T6's territory, not a T7 headline.** Dates: Member State wallets by end-2026 (Germany's own site says "by early 2027"), national relying-party registers from 2026-12-24, regulated relying parties obliged to accept by December 2027. [S]
10. **Sovereignty understated the hard artefacts and overstated openDesk.** Missing: SEAL and its 48 criteria, the €180M April 2026 award, CADA (2026-06-03), the binding SecNumCloud 3.2 decree (2026-08-12), the Data Act switching-fee ban (2027-01-12), the ODF/PDF-UA mandate (2026-03-18) and DINUM's 2.5M-workstation order (2026-04-08). Conversely openDesk is at ~80,000 licences, ~8,000 federal — the mandate is far ahead of delivery. [S]
11. **Galileo HAS phasing was vague and kit prices optimistic.** Phase 1 operational 2023-01-24; Full Service targeted Q4 2026; u-blox ZED-X20P-01B is the silicon milestone. Credible professional RTK kit is €3,000–5,000; ArduSimple boards are cheaper but integration-heavy. [S]
12. **Germany's company register is *not* HVD-compliant in practice** (60 queries/hour, no bulk, no SLA) — the draft assumed working HVD APIs since June 2024. Geodata *did* open on 2024-06-09 across all 16 Länder. This flips the opportunity from "consume the API" to "be the API". [S]
13. **Consumer agent-app distribution has an EU carve-out the draft missed.** Apps in ChatGPT launched for logged-in users **outside** the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, with monetisation limited to external checkout for physical goods. Do not plan an EU consumer business on that surface. [S]
