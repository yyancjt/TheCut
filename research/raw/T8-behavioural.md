# T8 — Behavioural, demographic, cultural, labour-market and geopolitical shifts in Western Europe (2023–2026), and the services they create demand for

*Research date: 6 September 2026. Part of the market-gap study in `research/00-brief.md`.*

## How to read this track

Claims are tagged **[S]** (cited search result), **[K]** (prior knowledge, unverified), **[I]** (inference). Almost all external pages were blocked by the egress policy, so evidence is search-result snippets, not fetched primary documents; the highest-stakes numbers are listed under "Not verified", and figures from commercial research aggregators are directional only. I avoid ground held by other tracks (T1 AI, T2 regulation, T3 energy, T4 hardware, T5 health, T6 money/identity, T7 substrate) and cross-reference instead.

**The one-paragraph read.** Western Europe in 2026 is not short of demand; it is short of *administrative and physical labour applied to household-scale life events*. Three demographic waves — death and inheritance, care, and business succession — arrive simultaneously in the countries with the deepest trades shortage and the slowest courts. Three regulatory waves — rent control, short-term-rental data-sharing and youth online rules — have turned ordinary households into compliance subjects. A fourth, softer shift — preparedness, "buy European", the physical resurgence of clubs and third places — created consumer categories that did not exist as purchasable products in 2022. The gaps are not software gaps but gaps in *orchestration*: someone who takes a messy household problem, does the paperwork and books the vans.

## Findings

### F1. The inheritance wave arrived; the binding constraint is administrative labour, not money

**(a)** German inheritance and gift tax revenue hit a record ~€21bn in 2025 on €141.1bn of *taxable* transfers, +23.1% y/y [S](https://www.newsworm.de/news/inheritance-tax-revenue-hits-record-eu21-billion-in-germany); total German inheritance is put at ~€400bn a year [S](https://www.ratgeber-erbengemeinschaft.de/erbengemeinschaft/immobilien-vererben-deutschland/), the European transfer at ~€3.5tn [S](https://ing.com/news/2025/the-great-wealth-transfer-europes-%E2%82%AC3.5-trillion-shift.html). The machinery is jammed: a March 2025 German Bar Association survey found **>40% of uncontested Erbschein cases take longer than six months** and ~70% of contested ones exceed twelve [S](https://rechtsanwaelte-gsp.de/2025/04/09/nachlassgerichte-unter-druck-die-problematik-der-langen-wartezeiten/). France runs a hard six-month clock with 0.20%/month penalties plus a 10% surcharge [S](https://www.ebene-avocats.fr/combien-de-temps-dispose-un-notaire-pour-regler-une-succession/), and Berlin startup tilde cites studies putting an ordinary undisputed estate at **up to 400 hours** [S](https://www.fu-berlin.de/campusleben/anwenden/2026/202604014-startup-tilde/index.html).

**(b)** The estate market is pitched as a *wealth* market (wills, vaults, tax planning); the evidence says it is a **deadline-and-logistics market**. Germany's own fix — notaries exchanging everything electronically with courts, registries and tax offices from **1 January 2027**, theoretically cutting land-registry entry from 4–12 weeks to under a week [S](https://germanpedia.com/digital-property-buying-process-germany/) — speeds the *notary's* leg and leaves the heir's untouched. That asymmetry is the opening. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Heirs aged 50–70, employed, rarely local, usually in an Erbengemeinschaft; ~54% of German estates contain property and **~120,000 inherited properties a year** have heirs who want to sell [S](https://www.ratgeber-erbengemeinschaft.de/erbengemeinschaft/immobilien-vererben-deutschland/), at a plausible €1,500–4,000 per estate — 2% of property-bearing estates is a €100m+ line in one country **[I]**. The UK is mature: **Settld** (acquired by Phillips Cohen June 2024, now NotifyNOW; 1,400+ organisations) and **Life Ledger** (past 100,000 notifications in 2025) [S](https://withfarra.co.uk/guides/death-admin-services-uk-complete-guide); Germany has **tilde** and **NachlassDialog** [S](https://www.nachlassdialog.de/). Gap: nobody in DE/AT/NL owns the *court-and-registry* leg alongside notification; nobody handles Erbengemeinschaft coordination, where a share typically realises only 50–70% of proportional value [S](https://deltalaw.de/erbengemeinschaft-immobilie-anteil-verkaufen/); nobody bridges to physical clearance (F2).

**(e)** High. v1 = structured intake, a per-institution letter/portal engine, deadline tracking, a human on the phone. RDG limits mean document preparation only, referring legal acts to a partner notary. Distribution is the hard part — buyers appear once — so start as the *supplier* to funeral directors, who own the moment of contact and have no software.

### F2. The physical half of the wave — house clearance — is unbrokered and now regulation-touched

**(a)** German clearance is a mature local trade priced at €20–50/m² in Berlin [S](https://immodo-berlin.de/blog/entruempelung-berlin-haushaltsaufloesung-guide/). Three changes landed around it: **separate textile collection became mandatory EU-wide on 1 January 2025**, with the revised Waste Framework Directive (EU) 2025/1892 in force from 16 October 2025 creating the first EU textile/footwear EPR [S](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/revised-waste-framework-directive-enters-force-2025-10-16_en); Italy launched a voluntary furniture EPR ahead of an EU requirement expected ~2027 [S](https://www.renewablematter.eu/en/new-life-furniture-extended-producer-responsibility); and Luxembourg measured **~11% by weight of bulky waste as directly reusable** [S](https://aev.gouvernement.lu/en/actualites.gouvernement2024+en+actualites+toutes_actualites+communiques+2025+04-avril+18-dechets-encombrants.html). Meanwhile the resale rail went industrial: Vinted did **€10.8bn GMV (+47%)** in 2025 [S](https://company.vinted.com/newsroom/financial-results-2025).

**(b)** Clearance is priced as *disposal* when a rising share of contents has liquid resale value on rails that didn't exist a decade ago, and EPR is about to make producers pay for the textile fraction. The right product is **valuation-first clearance**: photograph, triage into resale / donate / EPR / dispose, settle with heirs as a *net* number — sometimes zero, occasionally a credit. Nobody in the German-speaking market prices this way; furniture buy-back exists only as an upsell on a disposal job [S](https://www.wirkaufenmoebel.de/haushaltsaufloesung-entruempelung). **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Heirs (F1), landlords with a deceased tenant's flat, and seniors downsizing pre-death (German senior-move packages run €2,000–4,000 [S](https://sa-ex.com/aktuelles/)) — tens of thousands of jobs a year in the top ten DE/AT/NL metros **[I]**. There is no marketplace layer: heirs get 1–3 phone quotes with no basis for comparison; clearance firms have no demand generation beyond Google and no resale channel beyond a flea market. Missing: a **quote-and-settle marketplace with an integrated resale desk**, where the platform captures the resale margin and shows the customer the split.

**(e)** Medium. A real operations business — photos, triage rules, van partners, listing automation into Vinted/Kleinanzeigen/Catawiki, reconciliation — but startable with WhatsApp and a spreadsheet in one city. Margin comes from resale, not a take rate a tradesman will disintermediate. See T4 (cheap vision/VLMs make photo triage viable) and T2 (EPR).

### F3. Solo households, the 80+ curve, and the guardianship system

**(a)** 22% of the EU is 65+ (99m people, 1 Jan 2025), Italy 24.7%; old-age dependency is 34.5% EU, 39.0% Italy [S](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251001-2); the **80+ share is 6.2% in 2025, projected to 15.3% by 2100** [S](https://tradingeconomics.com/european-union/proportion-of-population-aged-80-years-more-eurostat-data). 16.2% of EU population lived alone in Dec 2024 (Sweden 22.2%, Germany 20.5%), and 4–5 in 10 Nordic *households* are single-person [S](https://tradingeconomics.com/european-union/distribution-of-population-by-household-types-single-person-eurostat-data). Germany runs **~1.3m legal guardianships**, shifting from volunteer to professional guardians, and the Zentrales Vorsorgeregister passed **7 million registrations, adding 391,848 in 2025** [S](https://www.bnotk.de/aktuelles/details/mehr-als-sieben-millionen-registrierungen-im-zentralen-vorsorgeregister-der-bundesnotarkammer), while Amtsgerichte are "massively overloaded" on guardian remuneration [S](https://betreuungs-experten.de/magazin/zwei-jahre-betreuungsrechtsreform-was-sich-seit-2023-wirklich-veraendert-hat).

**(b)** A registered Vorsorgevollmacht is a *pointer*, not a working system: the authorised person still proves authority to forty counterparties, tracks medical wishes and accounts for spending. 391,848 registrations a year are 391,848 households that just admitted the problem and already paid a notary — an unusually well-timed lead. And professional guardians are self-employed micro-businesses with court-set fees and nothing but Word. **[I]**

**(c)** Two buyers: the adult child managing a parent's affairs across cities (€5–15/month for authority + records + accounting **[I]**), and the **Berufsbetreuer** needing remuneration claims, deadlines and Vermögensverzeichnis production — thousands of them in Germany **[K]**.

**(d)** Consumer "digital will" vaults are crowded and mostly inert (DGLegacy, LegacyApp and a long tail; Europe ~27.5% of an aggregator-defined "digital legacy market" [S](https://www.kbvresearch.com/europe-digital-legacy-market/)). Nobody serves the *operating* phase between incapacity and death; nobody builds for professional guardians in DE/AT.

**(e)** High for the guardian tool — small, findable, homogeneous buyer with statutory workflows. Risk: it looks like B2B SaaS, so sell it as the guardian's service bureau (they outsource the accounting, you keep the software).

### F4. German care entitlements go massively unclaimed — a service business, not an app

**(a)** The Entlastungsbetrag rose to **€131/month (€1,572/year)** from 1 January 2025, from Pflegegrad 1, for home care; unused amounts lapse on 30 June of the following year, and the GKV-Spitzenverband estimates **hundreds of millions lapse annually**, with take-up ranging from a minority in Bavaria to ~55% in Saxony [S](https://www.pflege-deutschland.de/news/geld-leistungen/pflegegeld-2026-entlastungsbetrag-von-1-572-euro-bleibt-oft-ungenutzt/). The home-adaptation grant rose to **€4,180 per measure**, now including assistance systems such as fall sensors [S](https://pflegehase.de/wohnumfeld-verbessern-foerdermoeglichkeiten-2025/). Pressure rose on the other side: nursing-home co-payments hit **€3,364/month on 1 July 2026** [S](https://www.pflegeportal.org/blog/pflegefinanzierung/pflegeheim-kosten-2026-aktuelle-zahlen-und-wege-zur-entlastung-1669/), and care-dependency among the statutorily insured went from 4.6% to 7.6% between 2017 and 2024 (3.4m → 5.7m people).

**(b)** This is Germany's unclaimed-tax-relief equivalent, and it *recurs*: the same household under-claims monthly and the entitlement expires on a known date. A service that produces the claim, sources an approved provider (the money is only spendable with approved providers) and files reimbursement can charge on recovery with no cash outlay by the family — then sell the €4,180 adaptation, which routes to a tradesman. Rare case of a benefits-navigation business with a natural higher-margin second product. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** 5.7m care-dependent people, mostly at home, each with €1,572/year expiring; 20,000 households at a 20% fee on €1,000 recovered ≈ €4m/year **[I]**. Verbund Pflegehilfe is lead-gen, Nui Care an information app, Careship and Pflegix carer marketplaces [S](https://www.nui.care/) [S](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/pflegix) — none *executes the claim and the spend as one unit on a success fee*. Cross-ref T5 F11: one of the few reimbursed boxes a software-only team can touch, because it is administrative not clinical.

**(e)** High, but it is a service company with a software spine: you need a provider network approved under Landesrecht — rules differ by Bundesland, which is the moat. Start in one or two Länder. Stay clear of Rechtsdienstleistung.

### F5. The trades shortage crossed into a *business-extinction* problem, and succession has no working intermediary

**(a)** Germany is short ~200,000 craft workers on ZDH's measure; average time-to-fill went from **104 days ten years ago to 224 days**, with SHK at **246 days**; ZDH projects 500,000 missing by 2030 [S](https://www.heylead.de/ratgeber/fachkraeftemangel-handwerk-zahlen). The Netherlands is short 18,000+ electricians [S](https://hyteps.com/power-journal/shortage-of-electrical-engineers/); the UK loses a net 5,000–10,000 electricians a year [S](https://www.totalskills.co.uk/guides/electrician-demand-statistics). The apprenticeship pipeline is a *matching* failure: 2025 saw a record **39,900 unplaced German applicants (+28%)** while unfilled places fell 21.6% to 54,400 [S](https://aixformation.de/unversorgte-bewerber-ausbildungsmarkt-hoechststand/).

The sharper number: **up to 125,000 German craft businesses face succession within five years and roughly a third fail to find a successor**; 22% of all craft firms plan a handover within five years; nearly one in four owners is over 60; the top obstacles are finding a successor (57%), valuation (40%), tax (31%) [S](https://www.zdh.de/ueber-uns/fachbereich-wirtschaft-energie-umwelt/sonderumfragen/sonderumfrage-betriebsnachfolge-im-handwerk/). Europe-wide: KfW warns up to 190,000 German SMEs could close by 2026 for lack of successors; France has ~370,000 businesses in scope by 2030 against ~130,000 likely transfers (37,300 completed in 2024); Italy faces >1m by 2035 [S](https://blog.eilla.ai/europes-succession-crisis/) [S](https://ctacquisitions.com/guides/european-sme-succession-wave-2026-2030/).

**(b)** Everyone builds *software for* tradespeople into a market Angi/Instapro already owns (MyHammer DE/AT, Werkspot NL, MyBuilder UK, Travaux FR, Instapro IT — ~60,000 professionals, ~2m jobs, >€4bn gross market value) [S](https://www.iac.com/press-releases/iac-s-homeadvisor-acquires-myhammer-the-leading-home-services-marketplace-in-germany). Almost nobody works the *ownership* transfer, where the value sits. The official exchange, **nexxt-change, carries ~6,400 sale listings against ~1,300 buyer listings and mediates ~1,000 handovers a year** — versus 125,000 pending craft successions [S](https://www.nexxt-change.org/DE/Startseite/inhalt). A two-orders-of-magnitude coverage gap in a state listings board with no valuation, deal prep or financing.

**(c)/(d)** Sellers (60-something Meister who cannot retire), buyers (younger Meister, career-changers, small holdcos), and banks, Bürgschaftsbanken and Kammern who all want deal flow and have none; micro-M&A fees run 3–8% on €150k–1.5m businesses **[K]**. Concretely missing: **deal preparation and matching in that band** — accounts-to-teaser, a defensible valuation (obstacle #2), a financing pack, a curated buyer list — starting with SHK and Elektro in two regions. Guides exist [S](https://www.handwerk-unternehmenswert.de/wissen/betriebsnachfolge-handwerk); nobody productises the file.

**(e)** High, and unusually defensible for a tiny team: a relationship-and-document business, not a technology race, and boutique M&A will not descend below ~€2m enterprise value. Risk: long cycles; do not perform regulated tax/legal advice yourself. Distribution: Innungen, Kammern, Steuerberater.

### F6. Recognition of foreign qualifications is the labour bottleneck — and Germany quietly opened a second door in January 2025

**(a)** Germany positively concluded **~86,600 recognition procedures in 2025 (+10%)**, decelerating hard from +21% (2024) and +25% (2023) [S](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/08/PD26_295_212.html). Statutory limits are three months (four if regulated) *after complete documents* [S](https://www.frankfurt-main.ihk.de/aus-und-weiterbildung/ausbildung/ausbildungsberatung/auslandsaufenthalte-in-der-ausbildung/anerkennungsgesetz-5183972). The Chancenkarte issued **11,497 visas by 15 June 2025** (~18,000 expected for the year, 83% grant rate) [S](https://visaright.eu/en/blog/chancenkarte). Supply meanwhile collapsed: **German net migration fell 45% to 235,000 in 2025**, the lowest since 2020, and the population shrank ~100,000 [S](https://www.visasupdate.com/post/germany-population-shrinks-net-migration-plunges-2025-destatis).

The low-key change: **since 1 January 2025 adults over 25 with long craft experience can have competence validated and certified by a Handwerkskammer** ("Validierung der Berufserfahrung"), and BIBB adopted binding Teilqualifikation standards in December 2025, with a permanent TQ office and central database across 76 chambers [S](https://teilqualifikation.dihk.de/de/aktuelles/zusammenfassung-der-tq-veranstaltung-18-6-2026) [S](https://www.zdh.de/ueber-uns/fachbereich-berufliche-bildung/teilqualifikationen-im-handwerk/).

**(b)** Two doors now exist for people already in the country: full recognition (slow, document-gated) and validation/partial qualification (new, chamber-run, experience-based). The obvious population: 4.38m Ukrainians under EU temporary protection at 31 May 2026, extended to **4 March 2028** [S](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/07/15/eu-countries-agree-to-extend-temporary-protection-for-those-fleeing-ukraine-until-march-2028/); ~1.4m in Germany at ~50% employment, but **50% of men and 57% of women work below their qualification**, with recognition named as the main obstacle [S](https://tuenews.de/en/every-second-ukrainian-refugee-is-working/).

**(c)/(d)** Employers above all. ~352,600 foreign nurses work in Germany (20% of employees; 24% in home and residential care), and the state Triple Win programme delivered only ~6,000 people 2013–2025, so **most recruitment already runs through private agencies**, of which only 54 hold the RAL "Faire Anwerbung Pflege" mark [S](https://mediendienst-integration.de/news/auslaendische-pflegekraefte-und-aerzte-factsheet-zahlen-statistiken/). Agencies sell *people*; nobody sells the **file**. A recognition file factory — source-country document acquisition, sworn-translation orchestration (cross-ref T1 F9), gap analysis against the German reference profile, Anpassungslehrgang/Kenntnisprüfung planning, plus the new TQ/validation alternative — priced per completed file to the employer, is unbuilt at scale. Adjacent and equally unserved: telc B1–B2 Pflege preparation is a cottage industry of local language schools [S](https://www.telc.net/en/language-examinations/certificate-exams/german/telc-german-b1-b2-nursing/).

**(e)** High. Document logistics plus per-(profession × Bundesland × origin country) procedural knowledge, which is the moat. Ethical risk is real — build to the RAL standard from day one.

### F7. Dutch and Spanish rent regulation converted a rental market into a *transaction* market

**(a)** The Dutch **Wet betaalbare huur** (in force 1 July 2024, enforced from 1 January 2025) moved **100,000+ homes** from free to regulated, and landlords sold: 53,300 homes in 2024, **65,000 in 2025**, 7,840 in Q1 2026; Remaining free-sector rents rose from €16.81/m² to €19.17/m² [S](https://www.man-man.nl/verhuurders-verkopen-massaal-woningen-nederland-wooninfarct/) [S](https://beleiduitgelegd.nl/wet-betaalbare-huur-2026-complete-gids/). In Q2 2026 NVM agents listed **~56,700 existing homes, the most since records began in 1995**, and sold 45,200, explicitly citing continued *uitponden* [S](https://www.nvm.nl/nieuws/2026/nvm-recordaantal-woningen-te-koop-gezet-in-tweede-kwartaal-verkoop-blijft-sterk/). Spain shows the same physics: in tension zones, permanent-rental supply fell in Q4 2025 by up to 26% (Pamplona, A Coruña, Barcelona all double-digit) while **seasonal rentals reached 29% of the market, +36% y/y**, and Catalonia's Law 11/2025 extended controls to seasonal rentals from 1 January 2026 [S](https://www.eleconomista.es/vivienda-inmobiliario/noticias/13821032/03/26/la-oferta-de-alquileres-se-desploma-en-las-zonas-declaradas-tensionadas-segun-idealista.html) [S](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/05/30/894578-zonas-tensionadas-en-cataluna-se-reduce-el-mercado-de-alquiler-permanente). Dutch Wgv fines run €750 to ~€90,000 with licence withdrawal available [S](https://www.wijnenstael.nl/nl/updates/handhaving-wet-goed-verhuurderschap-wat-gaat-het-kosten).

**(b)** 65,000 Dutch sales a year are not normal sales. Most are tenanted apartments in the lower and middle bands sold by amateur landlords who need a WWS points verification, a legal route (tenant buyout, natural termination, sale in verhuurde staat at a discount), a tax view, and a buyer who is often the sitting tenant. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Exiting private landlords with 1–10 units (tens of thousands in NL) and sitting tenants who suddenly have a right-of-first-refusal moment and no advisor — €500–2,000 per unit for a compliance-and-route package, or a share of a brokered buyout **[I]**. Makelaars sell houses; nobody sells the *decision + compliance package* for the uitponden landlord, nobody represents the tenant, and nothing equivalent exists in Spain for the seasonal-rental temporality documentation Catalonia now demands.

**(e)** Medium-high; local-knowledge dependent (which is the moat), needs a partner notary. Political risk: the Dutch law has already been softened once.

### F8. From 20 May 2026 the short-term-rental host is a reporting entity — and the smallest hosts have no path

**(a)** **Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 applies from 20 May 2026**: standardised registration, platform verification and display of registration numbers, and monthly per-listing data to national Single Digital Entry Points [S](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1028/oj/eng) [S](https://chekin.com/en/blog/regulation-eu-2024-1028/). City enforcement hardened in parallel: Barcelona will not renew any tourist-flat licence after November 2028 (>10,000 affected; upheld by the Constitutional Court in March 2025) [S](https://www.idealista.com/en/news/property-for-rent-in-spain/2025/06/12/848193-goodbye-airbnb-barcelona-sets-2028-deadline-to-phase-out-tourist-apartments); Amsterdam cut caps from 30 to **15 nights/year in eight central neighbourhoods from April 2026**, with fines to €87,000 [S](https://shorttermrentalz.com/news/amsterdam-reduces-maximum-rental-limit/); Paris issued nearly €1m of fines in Q1 2026 with a 150-person brigade [S](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/france-airbnb-regulations-news/). Quietly compounding it: the **German hotel Meldeschein for German nationals was abolished on 1 January 2025** under the Viertes Bürokratieentlastungsgesetz, but municipalities levying Kurtaxe still need guest data — now under local Kommunalabgabengesetz rather than federal law [S](https://www.ihk.de/gera/wirtschaftsraum-ostthueringen/ihre-branche/tourismus-gastgewerbe/meldeschein-6311582) [S](https://www.tageskarte.io/hotellerie/detail/kurtaxe-tourismusabgaben-co-wie-hotels-ohne-meldeschein-zukuenftig-daten-erheben.html).

**(b)** Compliance software here is sold to *cities* (Granicus, Deckard, GovOS) or to professional managers (Chekin, Minut). The regulation redistributes supply toward operators with compliance infrastructure and away from those without [S](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/short-term-rental-2026-trends-guide-for-hosts/). The 1–3 listing host — most of Europe's supply by count — is the one about to be delisted; the small tourist municipality standing up a register is the other orphan. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Micro-hosts (€99/year is trivial against an €87,000 fine), the DE/AT Ferienwohnung long tail navigating a fragmented post-Meldeschein regime, and small municipalities needing a registration + Kurtaxe + statistics pipe they cannot buy from Granicus. Missing: a **micro-host compliance layer** — registration-number acquisition, per-municipality rules engine, guest-data capture satisfying both Kurtaxe and the surviving foreign-guest duty, night-count tracking against caps, and SDEP submission where the host is the obliged party.

**(e)** High. The rules database *is* the product; the code is trivial; the moat is that rules change per municipality per year. Risk: platform absorption (Airbnb could ship it free) — mitigate by also selling the register to the municipality. Cross-ref T2 (mandate calendar), T6 F8 (identity/age plumbing).

### F9. Household preparedness became a purchasable consumer category between March 2025 and April 2026

**(a)** The Commission's Preparedness Union Strategy (26 March 2025) set a **72-hour** household self-sufficiency standard and proposed a national preparedness day, with 30 concrete actions [S](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20250327-brussels-calls-eu-households-prepare-72-hour-survival-kits-attacks-disasters-conflict). Then it was executed: the Netherlands ran **"Denk vooruit"** from late September 2025, mailed a booklet to **8.5m households**, and measured the result — kit ownership rose **from 30% to 44%** and households with an emergency plan doubled [S](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/04/13/steeds-meer-nederlanders-bereiden-zich-voor-op-noodsituatie-door-denk-vooruit). Sweden re-issued *Om krisen eller kriget kommer* to 5m+ households; Finland and Norway ran equivalents [S](https://theconversation.com/keep-nine-litres-of-water-in-storage-how-baltic-and-nordic-countries-are-preparing-for-a-crisis-or-war-244783). Germany plans public shelters and a locator app with ~€10bn of civil-protection investment to 2029, against **579 operational shelters for 480,000 people — under 1% of the population** [S](https://www.iamexpat.de/expat-info/germany-news/germany-launch-bunker-location-app-amid-rising-global-tensions). The threat context is concrete: repeated Baltic cable incidents, the 16 November 2025 Polish railway sabotage, drone closures at Brussels, Copenhagen, Munich, Oslo and Vilnius, and Germany formally blaming Russia for the Leipzig drone attack on 1 September 2026 [S](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/01/europe/leipzig-drone-germany-russia-intl).

**(b)** A 14-point jump in Dutch kit ownership in one campaign season is **a category created by the state's marketing budget** — the rarest kind of demand event. And it is a *maintenance* category: water rotates, batteries die, medication expires, families change. Whoever owns the annual refresh owns the customer. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Households in NL/DE/BE/Nordics (44% of ~8.5m Dutch households already hold a kit, so the refresh market is millions of units **[I]**), plus a larger unserved second buyer — **employers and municipalities** with an implicit duty of care. Supply is a thin layer of one-off D2C shops plus Amazon generics [S](https://notfallrucksack.net/en/collections/all): no European brand does **kit + annual audit + replacement subscription + household plan**, no employer/municipal channel product exists, and the information layer is immature (Germany's shelter app is still a plan; schutzraum.app is community-built) [S](https://schutzraum.app/en).

**(e)** High for a team willing to touch physical goods: contract-assemble a kit, sell direct, and the software is a household profile plus an expiry calendar. D2C margins are thin, so the subscription refresh and B2B2C channel are the business. Risk: fear-marketing hazard — keep the tone civic and match government language. Cross-ref T4 (radios, power, sensors), T3 (home backup).

### F10. Defence and conscription-adjacent life admin: large, boring, underserved — but a watch item

**(a)** Germany's **Wehrdienstmodernisierungsgesetz entered force 1 January 2026**: service stays voluntary, but every male citizen turning 18 (from the 2008 cohort) must complete a fitness-and-willingness questionnaire, medical exams become compulsory from **July 2027** with 24 assessment centres planned, and targets are >260,000 active and **200,000 reservists by 2035** [S](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2026-01-28/germany-act-to-modernize-military-service-enters-into-force/). **(b)–(e)** A tiny team should not build weapons, but a compulsory questionnaire to a whole birth cohort, 24 new centres and a 200,000-reservist target generate large volumes of *civilian* admin, and the reskilling pipeline from automotive to defence uses the same welding and machining skills as F5. **[I]** The sharpest unserved niche is the *civilian employer of a reservist*, who needs absence and compliance handling and has no tooling. Feasibility is low-medium for a generalist, high with military background; the employer-side product avoids defence procurement entirely. Cross-ref T4 F12, which reaches the same conclusion from hardware.

### F11. "Buy European" is real, has no data infrastructure — and EU customs just made origin a price

**(a)** Salling Group (1,400+ stores, 34% Danish share) put **black stars on European-produced goods** across Denmark, Germany and Poland from early 2025 [S](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/food/danish-retailer-launches-label-to-help-shoppers-avoid-us-products/). ~40% of Danes reported cutting US purchases, European Alternatives reported a **1,100% traffic surge in 2025**, and **more than half of consumers in AT, FR, DE, IT and CH said they were likely to buy fewer US products** [S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_States_boycott) [S](https://tuta.com/blog/boycott-us-choose-european-products). Separately the EU **removes the €150 customs-duty exemption on 1 July 2026**, replacing it with a €3 flat duty per consignment (per product category for mixed parcels) plus a ~€2 handling fee expected ~November 2026; **4.6bn sub-€150 parcels entered in 2024, ~5.8bn in 2025** [S](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/e-commerce-150-eur-customs-duty-exemption-threshold-be-removed-2026-2025-11-13_en) [S](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/07/01/eu-slaps-3-duty-fee-on-shein-temu-and-aliexpress-imports). Salling built a proprietary flag; European Alternatives is hand-curated; the apps are crowd-sourced and unreliable. Meanwhile the de-minimis change makes origin a declared field on billions of parcels — data exhaust that did not previously exist. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Not consumers (extensions don't monetise) but retailers and marketplaces needing a defensible "European" flag, brands proving EU ownership in tenders, and public bodies — a licensed data product with an API. Between a proprietary star and a Reddit list there is nothing auditable; the valuable, unglamorous work is the **ownership graph** (UBO → brand → SKU → site), bootstrappable in one category. Cross-ref T6 F9: the EU Business Wallet giving company identity an API from 2026–27 is what makes it tractable.

**(e)** Medium. Data acquisition is the whole job; risks are accuracy/defamation exposure and political cooling. Hedge by framing it as a compliance dataset (origin, ownership, tariff exposure) with value regardless of mood.

### F12. Demand for gathering is rising while the venues that host it collapse

**(a)** Strava recorded **3.5× more running clubs in 2025** and participation +59% globally [S](https://sambadigital.com/the-running-boom-is-social-how-digital-platforms-and-gen-z-are-reshaping-the-sport/); parkrun attendance in mainland Europe rose **32.4%** [S](https://www.sgieurope.com/in-store/running-brands-bet-on-europe-to-win-the-dtc-race/122731.article); HYROX went from **570,000 athletes (2024/25) to ~1.5m (2025/26)** [S](https://www.sportspro.com/features/finance-investment/hyrox-business-model-mass-participation-private-equity-investment/); Europe has **51,000+ padel courts and federation membership +42% y/y** [S](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/12/online-the-fip-world-padel-report-2025-a-comprehensive-analysis-of-a-sport-in-constant-growth/); and German organised sport hit a **second consecutive record at >29m memberships** [S](https://www.dosb.de/aktuelles/news/detail/neues-freiwilligensurvey-sport-bleibt-vorreiter-trotz-sinkender-engagementquote). Dating went the other way: the global market recorded its **first annual revenue decline in 2025 (–2% to ~$6bn)**, Tinder –5.2%, Bumble –9.5% [S](https://www.businessofapps.com/news/dating-app-market-first-annual-revenue-decline/).

The venues are dying. Germany lost **~12,300 hospitality businesses in 2025, 2,905 through insolvency (+30% y/y)**, and in Brandenburg over 70% of pubs have closed in a decade [S](https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/handel-konsumgueter/restaurants-gastronomie-in-existenznot-12.300-schliessungen-im-jahr-2025/100209302.html) [S](https://jacobin.com/2025/12/german-pub-bar-closures). The UK lost roughly a pub a day with 789 operator insolvencies [S](https://www.uhy-uk.com/insights/pub-and-bar-company-closures-double-over-five-years-operators-battle-rising-costs-taxes). Berlin lost Watergate, Wild Renate and SchwuZ, with surveys suggesting nearly half of clubs have considered closing [S](https://www.berlintourism.org/berlin-nightlife-guide/); **50 Dutch festivals were cancelled in 2025** [S](https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/decline-of-music-festivals). Backdrop: **57% of Europeans aged 18–35 are moderately or severely lonely** [S](https://www.bertelsmann-stiftung.de/en/our-projects/next-generation-and-society/project-news/a-comparison-of-youth-loneliness-in-europe-in-2024), while German volunteering fell from 39.7% (2019) to ~37% and **59% of clubs are seriously worried about finding volunteers** [S](https://www.lsb-berlin.de/aktuelles/news/details/sportentwicklungsbericht-zeigtder-zustand-des-sports-in-deutschland-und-seine-herausforderungen-implikationen-fuer-berlin).

**(b)** The scarce good is not another community app: it is **a venue slot, a host who shows up, insurance, and someone to chase no-shows**. New formats are per-event and small; Meetup is a directory and Eventbrite a box office; the Verein layer is volunteer-run and shrinking even as membership hits records — rising load per volunteer, a software-shaped pain in a customer that does not buy software. **[I]**

**(c)/(d)** Micro-organisers running WhatsApp groups of 40–300 who lose money on no-shows (a €1–2 per-attendee fee is invisible), venues with dead daytime capacity, and Vereine needing member admin and event liability. Nobody has built the **host layer**: deposit-based no-show handling, venue-slot inventory, standard event liability cover, and a host/venue payment split, priced per event rather than per member.

**(e)** Medium; classic cold-start. Start single-city, single-format cluster, lead with venues where the pain is measurable (the empty Tuesday). Payments and insurance partnerships are the real work. Do not position as a loneliness app.

### F13. Youth online rules: the second-order market is the *substitute*, not the compliance

**(a)** **France passed a full under-15 social-media ban on 21 July 2026 with no parental-consent exception**: no new accounts from **1 September 2026**, existing under-15 accounts verified or deactivated **from 1 January 2027**. **(b)** Everyone will chase age verification (T6 F8 covers it). There is no European "13–15 year-old's phone" that is socially survivable at school; the adult analogue is Western European feature-phone sales growing ~4% in 2024 [S](https://www.silicon.eu/dumbphones-are-making-a-comeback-in-the-phone-market-16499.html).

**(c)/(d)** Parents (€80–200 hardware + €5–10/month) and schools, which every ban leaves with a physical storage-and-exceptions problem. A tiny team should not build a phone but can build the GDPR-clean coordination service on cheap Android with a curated device — or sell schools the unglamorous administrative side of a ban. **(e)** Medium: high scrutiny, strict child-data rules, free incumbents (Family Link, Apple). The schools-side product is the safer v1.

### F14. German childcare gains an entitlement in August 2026 that the system cannot staff — and the holidays are the hole

**(a)** The **Rechtsanspruch auf Ganztagsförderung began 1 August 2026** for first-graders, expanding one grade a year to 2029/30: up to eight hours a day, **generally including school holidays except a state-set closure of up to four weeks**, and youth-work offerings from recognised providers can now discharge the entitlement during holidays [S](https://www.recht-auf-ganztag.de/gb/eltern/rechtsanspruch-und-ganztagsausbau/das-ganztagsfoerderungsgesetz-223836). Meanwhile the Paritätischer Kita-Bericht 2026 counts **>550,000 unused Kita places for lack of staff and ~107,000 missing pedagogical staff**, with only 13.7% of facilities optimally staffed and projections of ~200,000 missing by 2030 [S](https://www.der-paritaetische.de/alle-meldungen/kita-bericht-2026-weniger-kinder-heisst-nicht-weniger-kita-krise). Germany also faces 40,000–80,000 missing teachers by 2030 [S](https://quest-eu.org/policy/teacher-shortages-in-the-eu-key-numbers-and-facts/).

**(b)** The law creates a *right* exactly where supply is thinnest and explicitly invites non-school providers — sports clubs, music schools, scouting, camp operators, freelance educators — to fill holiday weeks, with municipalities paying. Yet the sector is entirely offline: paper registration, Excel waiting lists, no cross-provider view, no capacity clearing. **[I]**

**(c)–(e)** Buyers: municipalities and Jugendämter (statutory duty plus budget), providers (needing booking, payment, Aufsichtspflicht documentation) and parents piecing together 10–13 holiday weeks a year. No national clearing layer exists — a boring, local, statutory market US startups will never touch. High operationally; municipal cycles are slow, but see the €50,000 direct-award threshold (F20). Safeguarding paperwork is itself part of the product.

### F15. Consumer legal tech's second wave is about claim *supply*, not another claims brand

**(a)** German collective redress finally moved: the Abhilfeklage has seen only **16 cases in two and a half years — but a third were filed in the first two months of 2026** [S](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/germany/consumer-claims-in-the-eu---what-can-consumer-facing-businesses-expect-in-2026), with RAD implementation running to 9 December 2026 [S](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/guides/the-impact-of-new-eu-mass-actions-directive-across-europe). Air-passenger claims remain the engine (~131,000 German airline proceedings in 2024), and Flightright launched **Allright** into employment, traffic and rent [S](https://legaltechcologne.de/blog/legal-tech-boomt-bei-fluggastklagen/). The **Digital Fairness Act proposal is scheduled for Q4 2026**, targeting dark patterns, addictive design, influencer marketing and subscription traps, against a fitness-check estimate of ~€8bn/year of consumer harm [S](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-protecting-our-democracy-upholding-our-values/file-digital-fairness-act).

**(b)** The consumer claim brand is a crowded, marketing-heavy business. The scarce input is **qualified entities' evidence capacity**: sixteen cases in thirty months against a framework built for hundreds is a supply failure. The buyer for evidence assembly — price-change monitoring, T&C diffs, cancellation-friction logging, energy-bill recalculation — is the qualified entity and the litigation funder, not the consumer. **[I]**

**(c)–(e)** Buyers: qualified entities, litigation funders and law firms; German legal-tech success fees run ~30% [S](https://www.legaltechverband.de/aktivitaeten/stellungnahme-zur-evaluierung-des-gesetzes-zur-foerderung-verbrauchergerechter-angebote-im-rechtsdienstleistungsmarkt/). ### F16. Cross-border personal admin became a mass-market problem with mass-market rules

**(a)** Switzerland has ~400,000 cross-border commuters, ~220,000 living in France; the France–Switzerland agreement's main provisions apply from **1 January 2026**, permitting **40% teleworking** without triggering French taxation — plus **new annual employer reporting from 2026 and first automatic exchange in 2027** [S](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/switzerland-france-40-percent-grenzganger-january-2026/). Luxembourg's thresholds sit at 34 days/year for FR/DE/BE commuters, and the EU/EFTA framework allows up to 49.9% cross-border telework without changing social-security subordination [S](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2024-218.html). And 4.38m Ukrainians must convert to national statuses before March 2028 [S](https://www.epc.eu/publication/temporary-protection-risks-ahead/).

**(b)** The 40%/34-day rules turn a *behavioural* choice — which days you work from home — into a tax-and-social-security calculation with employer reporting, for hundreds of thousands of ordinary employees with no advisor. That is a personal-finance product shaped like a compliance product. **[I]**

**(c)–(e)** Swiss employers now have a statutory reporting duty and employees will pay €5–10/month against a five-figure reassessment risk, across the FR↔CH, DE/FR/BE↔LU and NL↔BE/DE corridors. Expat-pension advisories serve the wealthy [S](https://harrisonbrook.com/best-international-sipp-providers-2025/); nothing serves the ordinary frontalier's day count or multi-country pension claim. ### F17. Local information is scarce, and trust in the national substitute is at a record low

**(a)** Around **4,400 German municipalities were forecast to have no local newspaper by 2025** [S](https://www.rsm.nl/information-for/journalists/in-the-media/detail/13171-forecast-news-deserts-by-2025-around-40-percent-of-german-communities-will-be-without-a-local-newspaper/). Supply-side saturation is measurable: **~49.9% of new English-language web articles in Q1 2026 were classified as primarily AI-generated**, up from ~10% three years earlier [S](https://thestacc.com/blog/ai-content-statistics/). **(b)** The market for local *news* is dead; the market for local **facts** is not — which school has places, when the Bürgeramt has appointments, what the council decided about the building next door. Feasibility medium; per-municipality data acquisition is the cost. Do not try to be a newspaper.

### F18. The climate protection gap reached households and SMEs — and Italy already made it compulsory

**(a)** Only about a quarter of European extreme-event losses 1980–2024 were insured (a **~75% gap**), and EIOPA's 2025 Eurobarometer found **only 17% hold nat-cat property cover**; EIOPA and the ECB proposed an EU-level scheme in December 2025 [S](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/insurance-protection-gaps-changing-climate-2026-04-16_en). Italy moved first: **mandatory catastrophe insurance for companies with Italian assets from 31 March 2025 (large), 1 October 2025 (medium) and 1 January 2026 (SMEs/micro)**, covering earthquake, flood and landslide, with non-compliance blocking public grants and disaster aid [S](https://global.lockton.com/us/en/news-insights/italys-new-natural-catastrophe-insurance-mandate-key-updates-and-deadlines). Heat is the other edge: EU air-conditioning penetration is ~19% (Italy ~56%), with units projected to more than double to 275m by 2050 and Brussels pushing passive cooling instead [S](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/07/02/brussels-supports-air-conditioningbut-not-as-the-main-answer). Energy hardship persists: 9.2% of Europeans could not adequately heat their home in 2024 [S](https://eu.boell.org/en/2024/11/20/what-status-energy-poverty-european-union).

**(b)** Italy just created by statute millions of small businesses that must buy a product they don't understand from brokers who can't service them profitably at that ticket. The generalisable pattern: **an address-level hazard-exposure assessment** (flood/landslide/seismic/wildfire), producing a document a micro-business hands its broker, is now a compliance artefact — and EIOPA's EU-level proposal suggests other states follow. **[I]**

**(c)–(e)** Italian SMEs and their accountants first (statutory, dated, penalty-backed), then brokers needing low-cost small-ticket service, then households in hazard zones where mortgage availability is at stake. Address-level hazard scoring exists in reinsurance and PropTech but not as a €50–200 Italian-language artefact mapped to the statutory categories. Medium-high feasibility: open hazard data plus a report generator plus an accountant channel; take care not to present a score as insurance advice. Cross-ref T3 F18.

### F19. Rural Europe: succession, predation and direct sales all crossed thresholds in 2025

**(a)** The average EU farm holder is **57**; 33% are over 65 against 12% under 40 — **more than five over-65s per under-35** — and the Commission's Strategy for Generational Renewal (**21 October 2025**) aims to double the young-farmer share by 2040 with succession as a named lever [S](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/building-future-eu-agriculture-young-farmers-focus-2025-10-21_en). **(b)–(e)** Farm succession has the shape of F5 plus land and CAP entitlements — now a funded Commission priority, so regional programmes will pay for matching and advisory. Medium feasibility; needs rural distribution and patience; best as an adjacency for a founder already in F5.

Germany produces 400,000+ estates a year, over half containing property, with 40%+ of uncontested Erbschein cases running past six months — and the only tools on offer are a UK-style notification aggregator or a document vault. Charge €1,500–4,000; sell through funeral directors, who own the moment of contact and have nothing to hand the family. The moat is procedural — per-institution requirements, per-Bundesland court practice, a partner network for the regulated acts. Deeply unfashionable, which is why it is open. *(F1–F3)*

**2. Care-benefit recovery on a success fee (Germany).** €131/month per care-dependent household expires every 30 June and hundreds of millions lapse annually. Establish entitlement, book an approved provider, file the claim, take 15–25% of recovery — then sell the €4,180 adaptation grant to the same household, routing work to a tradesman you can charge for lead quality. Not a care marketplace (crowded, low margin) but a claims-and-spend business with a physical second act, aimed at 5.7m care-dependent people. Start in two Länder; the Landesrecht provider-approval rules are the moat. *(F4, F5)*

**3. Micro-M&A for the trades.** 125,000 German craft successions in five years, a third failing, against an official exchange managing ~1,000 matches a year; boutique M&A won't go below ~€2m enterprise value, so the whole lower band is unadvised. Productise the file: accounts-to-teaser, a defensible valuation (the owners' second-biggest stated obstacle), a KfW/Bürgschaftsbank financing pack, a curated buyer list from Meister registers and career-changers. Take 3–8% on close; two trades, two regions, ten deals in year one. A relationship business with a document engine — exactly why two people can win it and VC-funded teams won't try. *(F5, F6, F19)*

**4. The recognition file factory, sold to employers.** Statutory clocks that only start on document completeness, ~1.4m Ukrainians in Germany with over half working below their qualification, 352,600 foreign nurses already employed — and, since January 2025, a chamber-run validation route for over-25s alongside a formalised Teilqualifikation framework. Sell employers a completed file at a fixed price: source-country documents, sworn-translation orchestration, gap analysis, route selection between recognition and validation. Build to the RAL fair-recruitment standard from day one. The moat is fragmented European procedural complexity — precisely what keeps this out of American hands. *(F6, F16)*

**5. Preparedness as a maintained household service, not a bag.** The Dutch state moved kit ownership from 30% to 44% in one campaign season; Sweden mailed five million booklets; the Commission set the 72-hour standard; Germany is spending ~€10bn to 2029. Supply is one-off D2C bags and Amazon generics. Build the annual model — kit, household plan, expiry calendar, yearly refresh subscription — then sell the same thing to employers and municipalities as a duty-of-care product with a preparedness-day hook. Start in NL or the Nordics where behaviour is normalised, expand into DE where state spend is largest. Keep the tone civic; the fear-marketing version will be regulated and reviled. *(F9, F10)*

**6. Micro-host compliance for the EU STR regime.** From 20 May 2026 registration numbers must be verified and displayed and monthly per-listing data must reach national entry points, against Amsterdam's 15-night cap, Paris's enforcement brigade and Barcelona's 2028 licence sunset. Every vendor sells to cities or professional managers. Build the €99/year product for the one-to-three-listing host — per-municipality rules engine, night-count tracking, guest-data capture satisfying both Kurtaxe and the surviving foreign-guest duty, submission handling — and sell the mirror product to small municipalities that cannot afford enterprise software. The ruleset is the asset; it changes every year, in every town, in six languages. *(F8, F20)*

**7. The host layer for real-world gatherings.** Running clubs tripled on Strava, HYROX went 570k → 1.5m athletes, German club membership hit a record 29m — while Germany lost 12,300 hospitality businesses in 2025, the UK a pub a day, and dating-app revenue fell for the first time. Demand for gathering is up; hosting capacity is down. Build the per-event infrastructure the new formats lack: venue-slot inventory for dead daytime capacity, deposit-based no-show handling, standard event liability cover, host/venue payment splits — priced per attendee, not per member. Lead with venues (their empty Tuesday is measurable), single city, single format cluster. Don't build a loneliness app; build the rails under the run club. *(F12)*

## Crowded / avoid

- **Another UK death-notification aggregator.** NotifyNOW (ex-Settld) and Life Ledger hold the integrations. Compete in DE/AT/NL/FR on the *court and property* leg instead.
- **Consumer digital-will vaults.** One-off purchase, no retention, and the moment of value is the one moment the founder can't control.
- **Trades lead generation.** Instapro/Angi owns MyHammer, Werkspot, MyBuilder, Travaux and Instapro across six markets.
- **General carer marketplaces.** Careship, Pflegix (Europ Assistance majority since 2022), Helpling-style models — thin margins, high churn, regulated labour.
- **Another Vinted or Back Market competitor.** Vinted did €10.8bn GMV in 2025; Back Market raised $510m. Build *into* these rails (F2).
- **Loneliness and friendship apps.** Real demand, no willingness to pay; dating-app revenue just fell for the first time. Monetise the venue and the event.
- **Padel/gym/court booking SaaS.** Multiple funded incumbents per country; the growth is priced in. **[K]**
- **Generic age verification.** The EU app and national wallets will commoditise it (T6 F8); the durable niche is the long tail of small sites.
- **Consumer claim brands (flight, energy, rent).** Marketing-intensive and capital-hungry. Sell *into* the claims industry instead (F15).
- **Hyperlocal ad-funded newsletters.** Repeatedly attempted, repeatedly unprofitable; the news deserts signal demand for *data*, not a revived local paper.
- **AI content detection.** A losing arms race (T1 F13).
- **Senior tablets and "simple" devices.** A twenty-year graveyard; the real gap is administrative, not interface.
